1. Overview

1.1 Project, (Its) Undertaker, and Funding (Nature of the Investment)
The project will be carried out in the southwest of Ulaanbaatar of Mongolia, composed of two parts: the Yarmag new bridge construction and the existing bridge rehabilitation project. The new bridge will be located 120 meters east of the existing one parallel to it across Tuul River. After the current project, the northeast side of the north bank of Tuul River will be connected to the city’s main road, Chinggis Ave., to the city proper. The northwest side will be connected to the existing urban roads. The south bank will be linked to the existing Ulaanbaatar Airport Highway to the south. The east will be connected to existing roads.

The project is funded by the concessional loan of the

government of the People’s Republic of China and the Capital Bureau of Public Road of Mongolia is the proprietor. The EPC general contractor is China Railway 20 Bureau Group Co., Itd.

1 Overview

1.1 Project Name, Undertaking Unit Name, and Nature of Investment Project

This project is located in the southwest of Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, and consists of two parts: the construction of a new bridge and the maintenance of the old bridge.The new bridge is located 120m east of the old bridge, parallel to the old bridge across the Tuul River. After the reinforcement and renovation of the new bridge and the existing old bridge, the northeast side of the north bank of the Tuul River will connect to the city’s main road, “Chinggis Ave,” leading into the urban area, while the northwest side will connect to existing urban roads. On the south bank, the road will connect southward to the existing highway leading to Ulaanbaatar Airport and eastward to existing roads.

The funding for this project comes from concessional loans provided by the government of the People’s Republic of China. The construction owner is the Capital Highway Bureau of Mongolia, and the EPC general contractor is China Railway 20th Bureau Group Co., Ltd.

1.2 Project Background, Research Process, and Construction Necessity

1.2.1 Project Background
Ulaanbaatar is flanked by rolling mountains to the north and south, with the clear Tuul River flowing gently from the eastern foothills of Bogd Khan Mountain in the south to the west. The vast grasslands to the east and west also shape the direction of the city’s development. The main urban districts are situated along the northern bank of the Tuul River, creating a long, narrow layout. To the west lies the Chinggis Khaan International Airport, while to the south is Bogd Khan Mountain, a sacred mountain in Mongolia. Ulaanbaatar serves as the national center for politics, economy, culture, and education. The transportation network primarily consists of railways and highways, supplemented by air transport, forming a well-connected transportation system. The Beijing-Moscow railway line runs through Ulaanbaatar, traversing Mongolia from north to south, reaching Suhkbaatar in the north and the Erenhot city in Inner Mongolia, China, in the south. This railway not only plays a significant role in Mongolia’s economic development but also serves as an essential part of the Eurasian “Continental Bridge,” connecting China, Mongolia, and Russia.

In a nutshell

The Yarmag Interchange, which crosses the Tuul River from the airport to the city center, was built in the 1960s and was designed as a two-lane, two-way road. It is a vital traffic artery connecting Ulaanbaatar’s city center to Chinggis Khaan International Airport and is extremely busy. However, the bridge structure is outdated, and its capacity has long been saturated. In light of this situation, a temporary steel bridge has been constructed to the west of the existing Yarmag Bridge, creating a four-lane bidirectional solution.

综上所述，寻求一个合理、可行的方案进一步加强Tuul River两岸的连接，改善市区至Chinggis Khaan International Airport的通行条件，成为了一个亟待解决的问题。
From the above-mentioned situation, it has become an urgent issue to formulate a reasonable and feasible scheme to strengthen the connection between both banks of Tuul River and improve the traffic condition from downtown area to Chinggis Khaan International Airport.

In summary, seeking a reasonable and feasible solution to further strengthen the connection between the two banks of the Tuul River and improve transportation conditions from the city center to Chinggis Khaan International Airport has become an urgent issue that needs to be addressed.
