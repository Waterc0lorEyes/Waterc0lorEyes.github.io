2024-12-20 · 翻译作业

长期照护（long-term care，简称长照）是人类社会进人老龄、高龄甚至超高龄社会的伴生制度，是走人长寿时代的人类社会不可或缺的基本公共服务制度。长期照护的概念起源于西方发达国家并得到广泛认同。长期照护的对象是由于 各种原因持续性地丧失活动能力者，而老年人是其中的绝大多数。长期照护的目标 是满足这些丧失活动能力者对保健和日常生活的需求，包括从饮食起居照料到急诊 或康复治疗等一系列长期的综合性服务。

Long-term care (LTC) is an essential public service system that arises from the aging population, including the elderly, very elderly, and super elderly, in human society as we enter the era of longevity. The concept of long-term care originated in developed Western countries and has gained widespread recognition. The primary recipients of long-term care are individuals who have persistently lost their ability to perform daily activities for various reasons, with the elderly being the majority among them. The goal of long-term care is to meet the health and daily living needs of these individuals, encompassing a comprehensive range of long-term services, from assistance with daily activities to emergency care and rehabilitation.

Long-term care is the system that emerges/comes into being when human beings enter aging, aged, and even super-aged society. It is a public service system indispensable in human society entering the era of longevity.The concept of long-term care originated from the western developed countries and has become widely accepted. The target of long-term care is those who have continuously lost their mobility for various reasons and the elderly are the absolute majority. The goals of long-term care are to meet the needs of those disabled for health care and daily life, including a set of long-term comprehensive services from diet and daily activities to emergency treatment or rehabilitation.

标准译文的伴生制度“is the system that emerges/comes into being”更直接地表达了“伴生制度”的概念，使读者更容易理解其背景。

“走入长寿时代”比起as we enter the era of longevity，ndispensable in human society entering the era of longevity更强调了长期照护在进入长寿时代中的不可或缺性，使得其重要性更加突出。

“一系列长期的综合性服务”encompassing a comprehensive range of long-term services和including a set of long-term comprehensive services的“a set of long-term comprehensive services”更具体，且“including”更适合引出具体服务内容。

分析：标准译文的“diet and daily activities”更全面地涵盖了照护内容，且“treatment”更符合医疗语境。

长照的兴起与人口老龄化有着最直接的关系。全球社会正在进人史上从未经历的人口迅速老化的时代，也即普遍长寿的时代。”这种变化是巨大且影响深远的。作为不可逆转的客观发展趋势，人口老龄化成为改变未来世界发展的基础性力量，是人类历史上前所未有的一场’无声的革命”。

This change is significant and far-reaching. As an irreversible objective trend, population aging has become a fundamental force driving future world development, representing an unprecedented “silent revolution” in human history.

2.1. Processes and tasks involved in PE

Krings’ study (Krings and Koby, 2001: 321-522) of PE processes in MT identifies a series of categories which help us define the subsequent PE tasks:

Krings(Krings和Koby,2001:321-522）对机器翻译的译后编译过程进行了研究，确定了一系列类别，有助于我们定义其任务：

· Source text-related processes. These refer essentially to reading the source text,either entirely or in segments with a view to recognizing some pattern for reformulation in the target text (morphological, syntactic or semantic) or for deciding on textual coherence. In the translation industry, the question of whether the post-editor should get access to the source text is still under consideration as in some contexts it is deemed as a barrier to reaching optimal productivity. In our experience,using the source text as a reference is key when dealing with low quality MT output for there are no other means of understanding (and thus correcting) the target text ·

●源文本相关过程：这些过程主要是指整体或分段地阅读源文本，识别需要在

目标文本中进行重述的（形态、句法或语义）模式，或确定文本的连贯。在

翻译行业中，译后编译人员是否应访问源文本仍存在争议，因为人们认为在某些情况下，访问源文本会影响最佳效率。根据我们的经验，在处理质量较低的机器译文时，参考源文本是至关重要的，因为在没有其他理解（从而进行修正）目标文本的情况下，这是唯一可行的方法。

Machine translation-related processes. This is one of the most important process categories, together with target text related ones (either in production or in evaluation)(Krings and Koby, 2001: 318). The category refers to reading the MT output either entirely or in segments, directing attention to elements which need further confirmation in the source text and evaluating whether a reformulation is necessary.As we will see below, the adequate performance of these tasks can be directly linked to instrumental competences that help the post-editor in appreciating what the machine is doing when it translates.

●机器翻译相关过程：与（译中或译后评估中的）目标文本相关过程一样，这是最重要一类过程（Krings和Koby,2001:318)。该类别涉及全面或分段地阅读机器译文，关注需要在源文本中进一步确认的要素，并评估是否需要进行重述。正如我们稍后所见，能否成功完成这些任务与译后编译人员的工具能力密切相关。具备了这些能力，译后编译人员就能理解机器翻译的运作过程。

· Target text production processes. By far, the largest proportion of the process falls into this category (Krings and Koby, 2001: 314), which is concerned with producing a new text either from old elements already present in the text or by adding new ones.Tasks involved in this process are then related to language correction according to PE guidelines (spelling, morphology, agreement, syntax order, lexical choice), dealing with terminology issues, guaranteeing style/terminology consistency/coherence. In this respect, we should remember that defining PE guidelines is a complex task since it involves considering quality acceptance from the client’s point of view, turn-around time and final use of the text, among otheraspects.Usually,approaches to PE distinguish between full and light PE (Allen, 2003; TAUS/CNGL, 2011) but, when human post-editors try to implement it in an actual PE project, they usually engage in full post-editing (O’Brien, 2011). As we will see below, this directly influences the definition of competences.

● 目标文本生成过程：这一类别在整个翻译项目中所占比例最大（Krings和Koby,2001:314)，涉及利用文本中已有要素或添加新要素来生成新文本。相关任务包括根据（拼写、形态、一致、语序、词汇选择等方面的）译后编译指导方针进行语言修正，处理术语问题，确保风格和术语的一致性和连贯性。在此我们应注意，定义译后编译指导方针是一项复杂的任务，因为它需要从客户角度考虑质量验收、交稿时间以及文本的最终用途等因素。通常，译后编译的处理方法分为完全译后编辑和轻度译后编辑（Allen,2003; TAUS/CNGL, 2011)，但译后编译人员在实际项目中通常进行的是完全译后编译（O’Brien,2011)。如下所述，这直接影响了如何界定相关能力。

Target text evaluation processes. Tasks involved in this process are related to making positive or negative evaluations of the MT output and to comparing it with the source

text, which, in turn, is related to defining quality in terms of client’s expectations.

● 目标文本评估过程：涉及任务是对机器译文做出正面或负面的评估，并将其与源文本进行比较，而这又与从客户角度定义质量有关。

·Reference work-related processes. This category is concerned with choosing dictionaries to be used (if any), collocations to be found, parallel texts to be consulted and/or informants to be asked for. Similarly, this involves tasks related to dictionary/terminology maintenance in MT and TM systems in order to improve lexical and phraseological accuracy.

●参考工作相关过程：此类别涉及选择可用的字典、查找搭配、参考平行文本并／或咨询信息源。同样，它也涉及机器翻译和翻译记忆系统中的字典／术语维护任务，以便提高词汇和短语的准确性。

·Physical writing processes. These refer to the action of writing linearly, deleting or inserting elements, leaving a gap, marking specific elements, overwriting and rewriting.

●物理（自然）写作过程：这些过程指的是线性写作行为，包括删除或插入元素、留白、标记特定要素、覆盖和重写。

·Global task-related processes. These are processes that serve to control the procedure of dealing with the PE task, i.e., they are related to task management the post-editor “must not only determine how the task should be best divided, but also in what order the subtasks should then be processed” (Krings, 2001: 510). This includes, as well, reporting feedback to allow for MT improvement and/or source content optimization, which can help solve repetitive mistakes in MT output Post-editors take then the role of stakeholder in the Controlled Translation scenario (Torrejón and Rico, 2002) and they can then realize that MT output improves thanks to their valuable feedback. Additionally, global tasks involve collecting samples of different post-editing issues in order to facilitate training of other fellow post-editors in the team, and keeping up-to-date with the latest advances in the field of MT and pre/post-editing tools.

●全局任务相关过程：这些过程旨在控制译后编译任务的步骤，即与任务管理相关：译后编译人员”不仅需要确定最佳任务划分方式，还要确定各个子任务的处理顺序”(Krings,2001:510)。这还包括提供反馈，以便改进机器译文和／或源内容优化，从而帮助解决机器译文中的重复错误。译后编译人员在”受控翻译”场景中扮演着利益相关者的角色（Torrejon和Rico,2002)，认识到，自己的宝贵反馈有助于改进机器译文。此外，全局任务还包括收集不同译后编译问题的样本，以帮助训练其他团队成员，并随时了解机器翻译以及译前编辑和译后编辑工具领域的最新进展。

使用“extort”一词，强烈地传达了对官员行为的不满，增强了情感的表达力度。
