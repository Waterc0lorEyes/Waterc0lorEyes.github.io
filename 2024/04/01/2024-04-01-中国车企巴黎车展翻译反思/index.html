中国车企巴黎车展翻译反思

2024-04-01 · 翻译作业

不同类型的文本对翻译策略提出了截然不同的要求——从神经科学文本的术语绝对精确，到新闻报道的动态叙述重构，每一次文本类型的转换都意味着翻译方法和思维的重新调整。本文以一篇脑深部刺激科学报道和一篇巴黎车展新闻的翻译实践为例，探讨科技翻译与新闻翻译的策略差异。

一、神经科学文本翻译反思

Translating neuroscientific texts like those on deep-brain stimulation demands a delicate equilibrium between unyielding terminological precision and the fluidity of natural language. This task positions the translator as both a linguistic expert and a knowledge mediator, navigating complex medical ethics and clinical narratives. The process hinges on several strategic pillars, beginning with terminological accuracy as the non-negotiable foundation. Adherence to established conventions——such as rendering “nucleus accumbens” as “伏隔核” and “deep-brain stimulation” as “深部脑刺激”——ensures scientific credibility. Crucially, term selection is guided by contextual nuance; for instance, choosing “欣快感” for “euphoria” effectively captures its pathological connotation, which is central to the article’s ethical inquiry.

Beyond individual words, successful translation requires the cultural-linguistic transposition of phrases and idioms to resonate with the target audience. Expressions like “nothing to write home about,” which lack direct equivalents, are creatively adapted into naturally sounding Chinese, such as “有所好转但仍不理想.” Similarly, preserving the raw immediacy of patient testimony involves shifting registers, transforming “It’s like being high on drugs” into the colloquial “感觉就像吸了毒一样” to maintain authentic dialogue.

Structural reorganization is equally vital. The complex syntax of scientific English often necessitates breaking down long sentences and making implicit logical connections explicit. Adding connectors like “（早在）” clarifies timelines, while translating “paralyzed by her expectations” as “被自己的期望困住了” more intuitively conveys psychological stagnation to a Chinese reader than a literal rendering would. Furthermore, the translation must faithfully convey affective depth and metaphorical power. Analogies like Dr. Mayberg’s “lifting your foot off the brake” (“把脚从刹车上抬起来”) are preserved for their explanatory clarity, and poetic patient descriptions are handled with care to retain their emotional weight.

二、新闻文本翻译反思

Translating the journalistic piece about Chinese automakers at the Paris Auto Show required a strategic shift from the technical precision needed for the neuroscientific text to a more dynamic, impact-driven approach suitable for news reporting. The primary challenge was balancing the original New York Times reporting style with the directive to adopt a “Chinese style” translation that could be “exaggerated” to highlight China’s strong presence.

A key strategy involved active voice conversion and subject prioritization. For instance, the opening sentence was restructured to make “Chinese automakers” the active subject (“面对欧盟……政策，中国车企……强势亮相”) instead of the more passive “China’s ambitions were on display.” This immediately centers the narrative on the actors. Similarly, descriptive phrases like “the displays that drew some of the biggest crowds” were rendered with more vivid, colloquial force as “人气火爆的展台,” effectively capturing the scene’s energy.

The translation also carefully managed tone and connotation. The word “boasted” presented a choice; translating it as “自豪地亮出” carried a positive, confident nuance, whereas a more literal translation could have implied arrogance. This decision aligned with the goal of projecting a strong, positive image. Furthermore, complex sentences laden with background information (e.g., the paragraph on European challenges) were broken down into shorter, clearer sentences conforming to Chinese news writing conventions, ensuring readability and logical flow. This process underscored that news translation is not just about transferring information but about reframing a narrative with a specific rhetorical force and perspective for the target audience.

翻译实践笔记

China’s ambitions were on full display

原文

China’s ambitions to become a force in the European car market were on full display this week at the Paris Motor Show, where a record number of the country’s automakers unveiled cutting-edge electric models despite a recent European Union decision to impose anti-subsidy tariffs on their vehicles.

译文

面对欧盟近期决定对中国电动汽车加征反补贴关税，本届巴黎国际汽车展上参展的中国车企数量仍创下纪录，各品牌纷纷推出前沿电动车型，中国进军欧洲汽车市场的雄心展露无遗。

译者注： 原文以抽象名词”ambitions”为主语，被动意味强。中文新闻导语应突出行为主体，将”中国车企”作为主语置于句首，化被动为主动。欧盟加征关税的政策背景作为状语前置，构成”尽管……却……”的转折结构。

The displays that drew the biggest crowds

原文

At the event, designed to showcase Europe’s top automakers, the displays that drew some of the biggest crowds were those from the likes of BYD, Leapmotor and Xpeng, which boasted how the speed of their technological advances——including the use of artificial intelligence——would help them compete with, or even surpass, their European rivals in the electric vehicle revolution.

译文

这场原本旨在展示欧洲顶级汽车制造商实力的活动，人气最火爆的展台却来自比亚迪、零跑和小鹏等中国品牌。这些企业自豪地亮出科技创新成果——包括人工智能的应用——欲与欧洲品牌在电动汽车革命中一较高下，甚至实现超越。

译者注： “the displays that drew some of the biggest crowds”译为”人气火爆的展台”而非”吸引最多观众的展台”，消除翻译腔。”boasted”根据视角选择译为”自豪地亮出”（积极自信）而非”高调宣称”（暗含贬义）。”including”在中文里可弱化处理，不必字字对译。

Protectionist efforts

原文

But Beijing is also eager to get in on that game, with the nine Chinese automakers at the Paris show appearing undeterred by what they view as protectionist efforts to slow their advance.

译文

但中国方面同样渴望参与这场竞争——虽然欧盟试图采取所谓的保护主义措施阻碍其发展，参展的九家中国汽车制造商却无所畏惧。

译者注： 原文”appearing undeterred by what they view as protectionist efforts to slow their advance”是一个长定语后置结构，译为中文时拆分为”虽然……却……”的让步转折复句。添加”所谓的”一词传达原文”what they view as”的立场标记。

From Rio to Paris

原文

At the BYD stand, a large-screen video displayed landmarks from around the world, from the Christ the Redeemer statue in Rio de Janeiro to the Arc de Triomphe in Paris. It was a visual reminder of the company’s ambition to make a Chinese car appealing to Western buyers.

译文

在比亚迪的展台上，大屏幕视频展示了世界各地的地标，其中包含了里约热内卢的基督像和巴黎的凯旋门。这一画面充分表明了比亚迪致力于让中国汽车受到西方买家青睐的决心。

译者注： “from … to …”结构译为”其中包含了……和……”而非”从……到……”，避免翻译腔。抽象名词”ambition”转化为动词”致力于”，”a visual reminder”译为”充分表明”，名词变动词使中文更流畅。

Acknowledged vs 坦言

原文

Renault’s chief executive, Luca de Meo, acknowledged the rising competition that Europeans face from Asian automakers.

译文

雷诺集团首席执行官卢卡·德梅奥坦言，欧洲车企正面临来自亚洲汽车制造商日益激烈的竞争。

译者注： “acknowledged”在新闻语境中译为”坦言”比”承认”更贴切——“承认”暗含勉强之意，”坦言”则更中性客观，符合新闻语体要求。

Compact electric vehicles

原文

He unveiled a line of several new compact electric vehicles, including one made out of 90 percent renewable materials, in what he said was “a manifesto for technological innovation.”

译文

他发布了一系列全新紧凑型电动汽车，其中一款车型的可再生材料使用率高达90%；他称这一系列产品是一份”技术创新宣言”。

译者注： “compact electric vehicles”规范术语为”紧凑型电动汽车”而非”小型电动汽车”。”in what he said was”处理为分号后的引语，结构清晰。

The challenge is formidable

原文

The challenge for European automakers is formidable. Europe is facing a drop in demand for new cars, with overall sales this year around 18 percent lower than prepandemic levels, according to the European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association. Electric vehicle sales are declining even more, accounting for only around 12 percent of market share this year.

译文

对欧洲汽车制造商而言，当前挑战十分艰巨。据欧洲汽车制造商协会数据显示，欧洲新车需求正持续下滑，今年整体销量较疫情前水平下降约18%。其中，电动汽车销量跌幅更大，今年市场份额仅占约12%。

译者注： 此段为新闻背景补充，中文处理时拆分为三个短句，每个句子传达一个独立信息点，符合中文新闻段落的节奏感。
