翻译反思录——从科技新闻到专利文献的翻译策略 Translation Reflections: From Tech News to Patent Literature

2024-04-01 · 翻译作业

本文将神经科学文本、科技新闻、行业规程与专利文献四类文本的翻译实践进行对比反思，探讨不同文本类型对翻译策略的差异化要求。

Translation Reflections

Translation is not merely the conversion of language but an art of finding the optimal balance between faithfulness, expressiveness, and elegance according to different text types. By analyzing the translation of a piece of tech news, an industry regulation, and a patent application, we can clearly see that professional translation requires adopting distinct strategies based on the core function of each text.

Translation of Tech News: Striking a Balance Between Accuracy and Appeal

The primary task of translating tech news is to accurately convey scientific information while ensuring the readability and appeal characteristic of the journalistic genre to attract readers. The translation of “Deep learning transforms smartphone microscopes into laboratory-grade devices” reflects this balance. For instance, changing the plain “转变” in the title to the more trendy internet slang “爆改” is a typical domestication strategy aimed at instantly grabbing the reader’s attention, aligning with the rules of news dissemination. However, such handling requires caution, as excessive colloquialism might undermine the seriousness of the science. Therefore, a safer approach is to use terms like “升级为” or “革新为”, which combine accuracy with impact.

In handling technical details, terminological precision is very important. Changing “解析度” to the industry standard term “分辨率”, and adjusting the description of the AI’s role from “生成” to the more technically fitting “重现” or “再现”, ensures the accurate transmission of professional information. Simultaneously, adjustments to sentence structure also serve readability. For example, changing the passive voice “被…替代” to the active voice “有助于用…取代” eliminates the translationese tone, making the text more aligned with Chinese expression habits.

Translation of Industry Regulations: The Paramount Pursuit of Precision, Standardization, and Formulization

Unlike the flexibility of news articles, the core of translating industry regulations is absolute precision, high standardization, and terminological consistency. Any ambiguity could lead to significant differences in practical implementation. The translation of “Railway Technical Equipment Management Regulations” fully embodies this principle.

First, terminological consistency is an iron rule. The translation of key concepts must be consistent throughout the entire text. For example, “铁路运输企业” and “施工单位” require the use of standard international engineering terms like “Owner” and “Contractor”, ensuring accuracy in professional discourse. Second, the force of legal wording must be accurately converted. The mandatory word “须” in Chinese should be translated as the standard legal term “shall” in English, or expressed in the passive voice. For the strongest directives like “必须”, they are accurately translated as “must” based on context, reflecting respect for the original text’s legal force.

Furthermore, the differences in sentence structures between Chinese and English are also very significant. Chinese often uses multiple short verb phrases, while English favors logical long sentences. Reducing the use of verbs, transforming multiple Chinese actions into English prepositional phrases, participial structures, or nominalizations, aims precisely to produce the rigorous, concise long sentences characteristic of English regulatory texts. The ultimate goal of regulatory translation is ultimate faithfulness, where all stylistic considerations must serve unambiguous and precisely implementable accuracy.

Translation of Patent Literature: The Deep Integration of Technical Accuracy and Logical Rigor

Patent translation is perhaps the most demanding field in terms of professionalism, requiring extreme precision in technical description, clarity of legal logic, and standardized use of patent terminology. In the translation of the patent for the cement-based waterproof membrane, the subtle differences in technical terms are the primary concern. For example, changing “flexibility” from the general “柔韧性” to the more professional “挠性”, and changing “rigid” from “过于刚性” to “刚性过高”, involves replacing everyday descriptive language with more precise technical terms.

More importantly, it is the grasp of the patent’s logical structure. It is necessary to clearly understand “comprising as component A)… and, as a separate component B)…” to show the parallel and independent relationship, accurately conveying the core invention point of “the physical separation of the two components”, which is the soul of patent translation. The choice of verbs also requires careful consideration. Directly translating “achieve” as “实现” can be awkward in Chinese; it should be changed to “获得” or “制备出”. Changing “applicability” from the vague “适用性” to the specific “涂抹性” or “施工性” directly addresses key issues at the application stage of the technology.

Facing the long, complex sentences characteristic of patent literature requires excellent handling skills. For instance, breaking down the lengthy sentence of the priority claim into three shorter Chinese sentences preserves all legal information while significantly improving readability. Patent translation is a deep integration of faithfulness and expressiveness, requiring the clear and fluent presentation of complex technical and legal information through clear logical layers on the basis of extreme accuracy.

Conclusion

The translation of these three documents vividly demonstrates that there is no universal translation that applies to all situations. Successful professional translation is necessarily built on a deep understanding of the text type, target audience, and core function. Tech news requires balancing accuracy and vividness, industry regulations pursue absolute standardization, and patent literature demands the precise integration of technology and logic. Only with this awareness of genre can translators make appropriate decisions throughout the complex process of language conversion and produce high-quality translations.

第一篇：深度学习将智能手机显微镜转变为实验室级设备

By Amy Akmal

原文

Researchers at the UCLA Samueli School of Engineering have demonstrated that deep learning, a powerful form of artificial intelligence, can discern and enhance microscopic details in photos taken by smartphones. The technique improves the resolution and color details of smartphone images so much that they approach the quality of images from laboratory-grade microscopes.

译文

加州大学洛杉矶分校萨穆埃利工程学院的研究人员证明，强大的人工智能技术——深度学习能够识别并增强智能手机拍摄照片中的微观细节。该技术显著提升了智能手机图像的分辨率和色彩细节，使其画质可媲美实验室级别的显微镜图像。

译者注： “enhance”译为”增强”比”改进”更准确，使微观细节更清晰。标题中”transforms”可译为”爆改”以吸引眼球，但需注意新闻严肃性的平衡，保守方案用”升级为”或”革新为”。

原文

The advance could help bring high-quality medical diagnostics into resource-poor regions, where people otherwise do not have access to high-end diagnostic technologies. And the technique uses attachments that can be inexpensively produced with a 3-D printer, at less than $100 a piece, versus the thousands of dollars it would cost to buy laboratory-grade equipment that produces images of similar quality.

译文

这一进展有助于在资源匮乏地区实现高质量医疗诊断，这些地区原本无法获得高端诊断技术。该技术所使用的附加装置可通过3D打印机廉价生产，单价低于100美元，而购买能产生同等画质图像的实验室级设备则需花费数千美元。

原文

Cameras on today’s smartphones are designed to photograph people and scenery, not to produce high-resolution microscopic images. So the researchers developed an attachment that can be placed over the smartphone lens to increase the resolution and the visibility of tiny details of the images they take, down to a scale of approximately one millionth of a meter.

译文

当今智能手机的摄像头是为拍摄人物和风景而设计，并非用于生成高分辨率显微图像。为此研究人员开发了一种可加装于手机镜头上的附加装置，能将拍摄图像的分辨率提升至约百万分之一米级别，增强微小细节的可见性。

译者注： “解析度”改为行业标准术语”分辨率”。

原文

But that only solved part of the challenge, because no attachment would be enough to compensate for the difference in quality between smartphone cameras’ image sensors and lenses and those of high-end lab equipment. The new technique compensates for the difference by using artificial intelligence to reproduce the level of resolution and color details needed for a laboratory analysis.

译文

但这仅解决了部分难题，因为任何附加装置都无法完全弥补智能手机摄像头图像传感器与镜头和高端实验室设备之间的质量差距。新技术通过人工智能重现实验室分析所需的分辨率与色彩细节水平，成功弥补了这一差距。

译者注： “reproduce”译为”重现”或”再现”，不用”生成”。

原文

The research was led by Aydogan Ozcan, Chancellor’s Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Bioengineering, and Yair Rivenson, a UCLA postdoctoral scholar. Ozcan’s research group has introduced several innovations in mobile microscopy and sensing, and it maintains a particular focus on developing field-portable medical diagnostics and sensors for resource-poor areas.

译文

本研究由电子与计算机工程及生物工程系特聘教授艾多甘·奥兹詹和博士后研究员亚伊尔·里文松共同领导。奥兹詹研究团队已在移动显微技术和传感领域推出多项创新成果，特别专注于为资源匮乏地区开发便携式医疗诊断设备与传感器。

原文

“Using deep learning, we set out to bridge the gap in image quality between inexpensive mobile phone-based microscopes and gold-standard bench-top microscopes that use high-end lenses,” Ozcan said. “We believe that our approach is broadly applicable to other low-cost microscopy systems that use, for example, inexpensive lenses or cameras, and could facilitate the replacement of high-end bench-top microscopes with cost-effective, mobile alternatives.”

译文

奥兹詹表示：”我们运用深度学习技术，旨在弥合基于廉价手机的显微镜与使用高端镜头的黄金标准台式显微镜之间的图像质量差距。我们相信该方法可广泛适用于其他低成本显微镜系统，例如使用廉价镜头或摄像头的设备，有望用经济高效的移动方案取代高端台式显微镜。”

译者注： “弥合差距”比”弥补”更好。”被xx替代”改为主动语态更自然。推荐译为”有助于用经济高效的移动方案取代高端台式显微镜”。

原文

He added that the new technique could find numerous applications in global health, telemedicine and diagnostics-related applications.

译文

他补充说，这项新技术可在全球健康、远程医疗和诊断相关应用领域找到广泛用途。

原文

The researchers shot images of lung tissue samples, blood and Pap smears, first using a standard laboratory-grade microscope, and then with a smartphone with the 3D-printed microscope attachment. The researchers then fed the pairs of corresponding images into a computer system that “learns” how to rapidly enhance the mobile phone images. The process relies on a deep-learning-based computer code, which was developed by the UCLA researchers.

译文

研究团队先后使用标准实验室级显微镜和加装3D打印显微镜附件的智能手机，对肺组织样本、血液和巴氏涂片进行拍摄。随后将两组对应图像输入计算机系统，训练其”学会”如何快速增强手机图像质量。该过程依托于加州大学洛杉矶分校研究人员开发的基于深度学习的计算机代码。

原文

To see if their technique would work on other types of lower-quality images, the researchers used deep learning to successfully perform similar transformations with images that had lost some detail because they were compressed for either faster transmission over a computer network or more efficient storage.

译文

为验证技术是否适用于其他类型的低画质图像，研究人员对因网络传输或存储压缩而丢失细节的图像进行测试，成功通过深度学习实现了画质提升。

原文

The study was published in ACS Photonics, a journal of the American Chemical Society. It builds upon previous studies by Ozcan’s group that used deep learning to reconstruct holograms and improve microscopy.

译文

这项研究发表于美国化学会旗下期刊《ACS光子学》，是在奥兹詹团队先前利用深度学习重建全息图及改进显微技术研究基础上的新突破。

第二篇：铁路技术设备管理规程（第一章）

原文

第1条 铁路的基本建设、产品制造应综合配套，保证质量，采用保证行车安全的技术装备，不断提高运输能力，以适应国民经济发展需要。应采用安全可靠、先进成熟、经济适用的技术，实现技术装备标准化、系列化、模块化、信息化，加快实现铁路现代化。

译文

Article 1 Railway capital construction and product manufacturing shall be comprehensively coordinated, quality assured, and equipped with technology that ensures operational safety. The goal is to continuously enhance transport capacity to meet the needs of national economic development. Safe, reliable, advanced, mature, economically viable, and applicable technologies shall be adopted to achieve standardization, serialization, modularization, and informatization of technical equipment, thereby accelerating the modernization of railways.

原文

铁路基本建设应严格按照国家规定的程序进行，必须符合国家相关法律法规，执行国家标准、行业标准和技术规范。设计工作应由具有相应资质等级的单位承担，根据已批准的可行性研究报告进行，并充分听取相关铁路企业的意见。设计文件应按项目审批部门确定的审批程序批准。

译文

Railway capital construction shall strictly adhere to the procedures stipulated by the state, comply with relevant national laws and regulations, and implement national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications. Design work shall be undertaken by units with corresponding qualification grades, based on the approved feasibility study report, and shall fully solicit opinions from relevant railway enterprises. Design documents shall be approved according to the approval procedures determined by the project approval department.

译者注： “单位”不可望文生义直译为”unit”，此处指承担设计工作的机构部门，用”department”或”section”更妥。”设计”在中文是名词，英文应转为动词。

原文

第2A条 本规程所称机车车辆包括铁路机车、客车、货车、动车组以及自轮运转特种设备。本规程所称铁路企业是指铁路运输企业以及铁路产权单位。

译文

Article 2A The term “rolling stock” (locomotives and vehicles) as used in these Regulations includes railway locomotives, passenger coaches, freight wagons, multiple units (EMUs/DMUs), and special self-propelled equipment. The term “railway enterprise” as used in these Regulations refers to railway transport enterprises and railway asset-owning units.

译者注： “自轮运转”指带轮子的自行设备，用”self-propelled”。”transport”一词需在全文保持统一。

原文

铁路运输企业是指从事铁路旅客、货物公共运输营业的企业法人，应按有关规定取得铁路运输许可证，按照许可范围开展铁路运输营业。铁路产权单位是指拥有铁路基础设施所有权的企业，可按有关规定取得铁路运输许可证，也可委托其他铁路运输企业开展铁路运输营业。

译文

A railway transport enterprise refers to an enterprise legal person engaged in the business of public railway passenger and freight transport. Railway transport enterprises shall obtain a railway transport license according to relevant regulations and conduct railway transport operations within the licensed scope. A railway asset-owning unit refers to an enterprise that owns the railway infrastructure. Railway asset-owning units may obtain a railway transport license according to relevant regulations, or may entrust other railway transport enterprises to conduct railway transport operations.

译者注： “营业”译为”operations”比”business”更贴切工程语境。建设单位用”Owner”，施工单位用”Contractor”，分包商用”sub-contractor”。”notify/inform”用于”通知”。

原文

第3条 工程施工应按照批准的设计文件的要求进行，严格执行工程建设项目招投标和监理制度。相关铁路企业应会同工程设计、施工单位制定安全施工方案，按照方案进行施工，加强环境保护，确保工程质量。施工完毕应及时清理现场，不得影响铁路运营安全。

译文

Article 3 Engineering construction shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of the approved design documents, strictly implementing the bidding and supervision systems for construction projects. The relevant railway enterprises shall, in conjunction with the engineering design and construction units, formulate a safe construction plan, construct according to the plan, strengthen environmental protection, and ensure project quality. Upon completion of construction, the site shall be promptly cleaned up without affecting the safety of railway operations.

译者注： 中文动词堆砌较多，英文应尽量精简动词数量。

原文

涉及营业线施工时，应按铁路企业规定程序审批，且应保证行车安全，减少对运输的影响。

译文

When construction involves existing railway lines, it shall be approved according to the procedures stipulated by the railway enterprise, ensuring traffic safety and minimizing the impact on transportation.

原文

第4条 新建、改建工程竣工后，应按规定进行验收和安全评估。线路验收时应达到设计速度。在确认工程符合技术标准、设计文件的要求，并检查竣工文件和技术设备使用说明书等资料齐全后，方可交接。新建、改建的工程设施应有明确的质量保证期。如运输生产急需，可按上述原则分段验收交接。

译文

Article 4 Upon completion of new or renovated projects, acceptance checks and safety assessments shall be conducted in accordance with regulations. During track acceptance, the design speed shall be achieved. The handover can only take place after verifying that the project complies with technical standards and the requirements of the design documents, and confirming that the completion documents and technical equipment manuals are complete. New or renovated engineering facilities shall have a clearly defined warranty period. If urgently needed for transport operations, sectional acceptance and handover may be conducted in accordance with the above principles.

译者注： “分段验收”需在译文中明确体现。”线路验收时应达到设计速度”要强调线路与速度的因果关系。

第三篇：水泥基防水涂料专利翻译

原文

This is a non-provisional application that claims the benefit of U.S. Provisional Application 62/011227 filed 06/12/2014, the entire contents of which are hereby incorporated by reference.

译文

本申请是非临时申请，其主张2014年6月12日申请的美国临时申请62/011227的优先权，该临时申请的全部内容在此通过引用并入本文。

译者注： 原文一句过长，宜断为三部分，使中文逻辑清晰。

原文

The present invention relates to two-component compositions for use in making cementitious waterproofing membranes comprising as component A) one or more acrylic aqueous emulsion copolymer having a measured glass transition temperature (Tg) of from -40 to 0 °C, preferably -20 to 0 °C, and comprising the residue of a reducing agent, and, as a separate component B) a fast curing dry mix powder composition of a hydraulic cement and a high alumina content cement.

译文

本发明涉及用于制备水泥基防水涂料的双组分组合物，其包含：作为组分A）一种或多种丙烯酸水性乳液共聚物，其测量玻璃化转变温度(Tg)为-40℃至0℃，优选-20℃至0℃，并且包含还原剂残基；以及作为独立组分B）的快固化干混粉末组合物，其包含水硬性水泥和高铝水泥。

译者注： “comprising as component A)… and, as a separate component B)…” 译为”作为组分A…以及作为独立组分B”，凸显并列关系和双组分的独立性。

原文

More particularly, it relates to two component compositions wherein the A) aqueous emulsion copolymer is the copolymerization product of (i) from 60 to 89.9 wt.% of one or more nonionic (meth)acrylic monomers, (ii) from 10 to 40 wt.% of one or more vinyl aromatic monomers, (iii) from 0.1 to 2.0 wt.% of one or more monomers chosen from itaconic acid, methacrylic acid, amides of a,β-unsaturated C3 to C6 carboxylic acids, and mixtures thereof, all wt.%s of monomers based on the total monomer solids.

译文

更具体地，本发明涉及双组分组合物，其中所述A）水性乳液共聚物是以下单体的共聚产物：(i)60重量%至89.9重量%的一种或多种非离子性（甲基）丙烯酸酯单体，(ii)10重量%至40重量%的一种或多种乙烯基芳香族单体，(iii)0.1重量%至2.0重量%的一种或多种选自衣康酸、甲基丙烯酸、a,β-不饱和C3至C6羧酸酰胺及其混合物的单体，所有单体重量百分比均基于单体总固体量。

原文

Waterproofing membranes find use as the support and sealer layer underneath tiles in bathrooms, terraces, swimming pools and water tanks. In ordinary Portland cement (OPC) or standard two-component cementitious waterproofing membrane compositions, 2 mortar layers have to be applied to achieve sufficient thickness and waterproofing quality. When using OPC in a dry mix, the time to apply the second layer is after at least 24 hours.

译文

防水涂料用作浴室、露台、游泳池和水箱中瓷砖下方的支撑层和密封层。在使用普通硅酸盐水泥（OPC）或标准双组分水泥基防水涂料时，需要涂抹两层砂浆才能达到足够的厚度和防水质量。采用OPC干混料时，第二层砂浆的施工需等待至少24小时后进行。

原文

Faster curing waterproofing membranes can be achieved by using fast setting cement calcium alumina cement (CAC). However, there are multiple challenges when using emulsion polymers in fast setting cement compositions. As the wet mortar thickens very fast, the applicability and workability become very difficult; pot life is unacceptably short and the resulting waterproofing membrane is too rigid, and thereby lacks flexibility and often cracks so that it is not waterproof.

译文

通过使用快凝高铝水泥（CAC）可以制备快速固化的防水涂料。然而，在快凝水泥组合物中使用乳液聚合物存在多重挑战。由于湿砂浆快速变稠，其涂抹性和可施工性变得非常困难；适用期短得无法接受，并且所得防水涂料刚性过高，因此缺乏柔韧性，经常开裂导致无法防水。

译者注： “achieve”不宜直译为”实现”，用”获得””制备”更佳。”applicability”译为”涂抹性”或”施工性”而非”适用性”。”flexibility”在材料学中译为”挠性”而非”柔韧性”。”刚性”是形容词，”rigid”译为”刚性过高”。

原文

Further, in fast setting waterproofing membrane compositions, the emulsion polymer does not provide enough flexibility to give sufficient crack bridging in dry/wet conditions. One very expensive way to solve the problem of the rigidity of the resulting waterproofing membrane would be to reduce the glass transition temperature (Tg) of the emulsion polymer, making it softer and more flexible, and to increase significantly the polymer to cement ratio.

译文

此外，在快凝防水涂料组合物中，乳液聚合物无法提供足够的挠性以满足干/湿条件下的充分裂纹桥接能力。解决所得防水涂料刚性问题的一种成本非常高的方法是降低乳液聚合物的玻璃化转变温度（Tg）使其更软、更柔韧，并显著提高聚合物与水泥的比例。
