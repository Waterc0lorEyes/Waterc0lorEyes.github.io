翻译反思录（一）——从科技新闻到专利文献的翻译策略

2024-01-15 · 翻译作业

翻译并非单纯的语言转换，而是根据文本类型，在”信””达””雅”之间寻找最佳平衡的艺术。本文通过分析一篇科技新闻、一部行业规程和一项专利申请的翻译实践，探讨不同文本类型对翻译策略的差异化要求。

Translation Reflections

Translation is not merely the conversion of language but an art of finding the optimal balance between faithfulness, expressiveness, and elegance according to different text types. By analyzing the translation of a piece of tech news, an industry regulation, and a patent application, we can clearly see that professional translation requires adopting distinct strategies based on the core function of each text.

Translation of Tech News: Striking a Balance Between Accuracy and Appeal

The primary task of translating tech news is to accurately convey scientific information while ensuring the readability and appeal characteristic of the journalistic genre to attract readers.

The translation of “Deep learning transforms smartphone microscopes into laboratory-grade devices” reflects this balance. For instance, changing the plain “转变” in the title to the more trendy internet slang “爆改” is a typical domestication strategy aimed at instantly grabbing the reader’s attention, aligning with the rules of news dissemination. However, such handling requires caution, as excessive colloquialism might undermine the seriousness of the science. Therefore, a safer approach is to use terms like “升级为” or “革新为”, which combine accuracy with impact.

In handling technical details, terminological precision is very important. Changing “解析度” to the industry standard term “分辨率”, and adjusting the description of the AI’s role from “生成” to the more technically fitting “重现” or “再现”, ensures the accurate transmission of professional information. Simultaneously, adjustments to sentence structure also serve readability. For example, changing the passive voice “被……替代” to the active voice “有助于用……取代” eliminates the translationese tone, making the text more aligned with Chinese expression habits. At the same time, phrases like “找到广泛应用” should be more specific to make the translation more fluent and idiomatic. These considerations collectively point to one goal: ensuring the scientific faithfulness as the foundation, achieving expressiveness in meaning, and appropriately pursuing the elegance required by the news genre.

Translation of Industry Regulations: The Paramount Pursuit of Precision, Standardization, and Formulization

Unlike the flexibility of news articles, the core of translating industry regulations is absolute precision, high standardization, and terminological consistency. Any ambiguity could lead to significant differences in practical implementation.

The translation of “Railway Technical Equipment Management Regulations” fully embodies this principle. First, terminological consistency is an iron rule. The translation of key concepts must be consistent throughout the entire text. For example, “铁路运输企业” and “施工单位” suggesting the use of standard international engineering terms like “Owner” and “Contractor.” This ensures accuracy in professional discourse. Second, the force of legal wording must be accurately converted. The mandatory word “须” in Chinese should be translated as the standard legal term “shall” in English, or expressed in the passive voice. This treatment does not dilute the original meaning but adheres to the norms of legal English. For the strongest directives like “必须”, they are accurately translated as “must” based on context, reflecting respect for the original text’s legal force.

Furthermore, the differences in sentence structures between Chinese and English are also very significant. Chinese often uses multiple short verb phrases, while English favors logical long sentences. The suggestion to reduce the use of verbs, transforming multiple Chinese actions into English prepositional phrases, participial structures, or nominalizations, aims precisely to produce the rigorous, concise long sentences characteristic of English regulatory texts. For key information like “分段验收”, it is necessary to ensure that the translation is not omitted. All this indicates that the ultimate goal of regulatory translation is ultimate faithfulness, where all stylistic considerations must serve unambiguous and precisely implementable accuracy.

Translation of Patent Literature: The Deep Integration of Technical Accuracy and Logical Rigor

Patent translation is perhaps the most demanding field in terms of professionalism, requiring extreme precision in technical description, clarity of legal logic, and standardized use of patent terminology.

In the translation of the patent for the cement-based waterproof membrane, the subtle differences in technical terms are the primary concern. For example, changing “flexibility” from the general “柔韧性” to the more professional “挠性”, and changing “rigid” from “过于刚性” to “刚性过高”, involves replacing everyday descriptive language with more precise technical terms. More importantly, it’s the grasp of the patent’s logical structure. It is necessary to clearly understand “comprising as component A)…… and, as a separate component B)……” this structure to show the parallel and independent relationship of “as component A” and “as a separate component B”. This accurately conveys the core invention point of “the physical separation of the two components”, which is the soul of patent translation.

The choice of verbs also requires careful consideration. It’s noted that directly translating “achieve” as “实现” can be awkward in Chinese; it should be changed to “获得” or “制备出”. Changing “applicability” from the vague “适用性” to the specific “涂抹性” or “施工性” directly addresses key issues at the application stage of the technology. These adjustments all make the communication of technical content more precise. Facing the long, complex sentences characteristic of patent literature, requires excellent handling skills. For instance, breaking down the lengthy sentence of the priority claim into three shorter Chinese sentences——“本申请是……”, “其主张……”, “……内容并入……”——preserves all legal information while significantly improving readability. It can be said that patent translation is a deep integration of faithfulness and expressiveness, requiring the clear and fluent presentation of complex technical and legal information through clear logical layers on the basis of extreme accuracy.

Conclusion

In summary, the translation of these three documents vividly demonstrates that there is no universal translation that applies to all situations. Successful professional translation is necessarily built on a deep understanding of the text type, target audience, and core function. Tech news requires balancing accuracy and vividness, industry regulations pursue absolute standardization, and patent literature demands the precise integration of technology and logic. Only with this awareness of genre can translators make appropriate decisions throughout the complex process of language conversion and produce high-quality translations.

翻译笔记

第一篇 · 深度学习爆改智能手机显微镜

原文

Deep learning transforms smartphone microscopes into laboratory-grade devices

译文

深度学习将智能手机显微镜升级为实验室级别设备

译者注： 本文为科技新闻，标题可适当夸张以吸引眼球。”转变”可改为”爆改”更吸睛，但需注意不过度口语化。”升级为”或”革新为”是兼顾准确与冲击力的安全选择。

原文

Researchers at the UCLA Samueli School of Engineering have demonstrated that deep learning, a powerful form of artificial intelligence, can discern and enhance microscopic details in photos taken by smartphones. The technique improves the resolution and color details of smartphone images so much that they approach the quality of images from laboratory-grade microscopes.

译文

加州大学洛杉矶分校萨穆埃利工程学院的研究人员证明，强大的人工智能技术——深度学习能够识别并增强智能手机拍摄照片中的微观细节。该技术显著提升了智能手机图像的分辨率和色彩细节，使其画质可媲美实验室级别的显微镜图像。

译者注： “enhance” 译为”增强”，使微观细节更清晰。原文”enhance microscopic details”强调对细节的强化，不同于简单的”improve”，”增强”更能体现 AI 对细节的放大与锐化。

原文

The advance could help bring high-quality medical diagnostics into resource-poor regions, where people otherwise do not have access to high-end diagnostic technologies. And the technique uses attachments that can be inexpensively produced with a 3-D printer, at less than $100 a piece, versus the thousands of dollars it would cost to buy laboratory-grade equipment that produces images of similar quality.

译文

这一进展有助于在资源匮乏地区实现高质量医疗诊断，这些地区原本无法获得高端诊断技术。该技术所使用的附加装置可通过 3D 打印机廉价生产，单价低于 100 美元，而购买能产生同等画质图像的实验室级设备则需花费数千美元。

原文

Cameras on today’s smartphones are designed to photograph people and scenery, not to produce high-resolution microscopic images. So the researchers developed an attachment that can be placed over the smartphone lens to increase the resolution and the visibility of tiny details of the images they take, down to a scale of approximately one millionth of a meter.

译文

当今智能手机的摄像头是为拍摄人物和风景而设计，并非用于生成高分辨率显微图像。为此，研究人员开发了一种可加装于手机镜头上的附加装置，能将拍摄图像的分辨率提升至约百万分之一米级别，增强微小细节的可见性。

译者注： “解析度”应改为行业标准术语”分辨率”。”millionth of a meter”即”微米级”，但原文未使用”micron”一词，保留”百万分之一米”以忠实。

原文

But that only solved part of the challenge, because no attachment would be enough to compensate for the difference in quality between smartphone cameras’ image sensors and lenses and those of high-end lab equipment. The new technique compensates for the difference by using artificial intelligence to reproduce the level of resolution and color details needed for a laboratory analysis.

译文

但这仅解决了部分难题，因为任何附加装置都无法完全弥补智能手机摄像头图像传感器与镜头同高端实验室设备之间的质量差距。新技术通过人工智能重现实验室分析所需的分辨率与色彩细节水平，成功弥补了这一差距。

译者注： “reproduce”应译为”重现”或”再现”，而非”生成”。”生成”暗示从无到有，而”重现”更准确地传达 AI 基于输入图像重建细节的过程。

原文

“Using deep learning, we set out to bridge the gap in image quality between inexpensive mobile phone-based microscopes and gold-standard bench-top microscopes that use high-end lenses,” Ozcan said.

译文

奥兹詹表示：”我们运用深度学习技术，旨在弥合基于廉价手机的显微镜与使用高端镜头的黄金标准台式显微镜之间的图像质量差距。”

译者注： “bridge the gap”译为”弥合差距”，搭配优于”弥补差距”。”弥合”强调将裂痕缝合，更符合原文”bridge”的隐喻。

原文

“We believe that our approach is broadly applicable to other low-cost microscopy systems that use, for example, inexpensive lenses or cameras, and could facilitate the replacement of high-end bench-top microscopes with cost-effective, mobile alternatives.”

译文

“我们相信该方法可广泛适用于其他低成本显微镜系统，例如使用廉价镜头或摄像头的设备，有望以经济高效的移动替代方案取代高端台式显微镜。”

译者注： 原文被动语态 “be replaced by” 改为中文主动语态 “以……取代……”，消除翻译腔。”cost-effective, mobile alternatives” 译为”经济高效的移动替代方案”。

原文

The research was led by Aydogan Ozcan, Chancellor’s Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Bioengineering, and Yair Rivenson, a UCLA postdoctoral scholar.

译文

本研究由电子与计算机工程及生物工程系特聘教授艾多甘·奥兹詹和博士后研究员亚伊尔·里文松共同领导。

原文

The researchers shot images of lung tissue samples, blood and Pap smears, first using a standard laboratory-grade microscope, and then with a smartphone with the 3D-printed microscope attachment. The researchers then fed the pairs of corresponding images into a computer system that “learns” how to rapidly enhance the mobile phone images. The process relies on a deep-learning-based computer code, which was developed by the UCLA researchers.

译文

研究团队先后使用标准实验室级显微镜和加装 3D 打印显微镜附件的智能手机，对肺组织样本、血液和巴氏涂片进行拍摄。随后将两组对应图像输入计算机系统，训练其”学会”如何快速增强手机图像质量。该过程依托于加州大学洛杉矶分校研究人员开发的基于深度学习的计算机代码。

原文

The study was published in ACS Photonics, a journal of the American Chemical Society. It builds upon previous studies by Ozcan’s group that used deep learning to reconstruct holograms and improve microscopy.

译文

这项研究发表于美国化学会旗下期刊《ACS 光子学》，是在奥兹詹团队先前利用深度学习重建全息图及改进显微技术研究基础上的新突破。

译者注： 这句可与前文交换顺序以优化中文语流。原文”builds upon”译为”是在……基础上的新突破”，既传达承继关系，又突出创新性。

第二篇 · 铁路技术设备管理规程（节选）

原文

第一编 技术设备 / 第一章 基本要求

Railway capital construction and product manufacturing shall be comprehensively coordinated, quality assured, and equipped with technology that ensures operational safety. The goal is to continuously enhance transport capacity to meet the needs of national economic development.

Safe, reliable, advanced, mature, economically viable, and applicable technologies shall be adopted to achieve standardization, serialization, modularization, and informatization of technical equipment, thereby accelerating the modernization of railways.

译文

铁路的基本建设、产品制造应综合配套，保证质量，采用保证行车安全的技术装备，不断提高运输能力，以适应国民经济发展需要。应采用安全可靠、先进成熟、经济适用的技术，实现技术装备标准化、系列化、模块化、信息化，加快实现铁路现代化。

原文

Railway capital construction shall strictly adhere to the procedures stipulated by the state, comply with relevant national laws and regulations, and implement national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications.

Design work shall be undertaken by units with corresponding qualification grades, based on the approved feasibility study report, and shall fully solicit opinions from relevant railway enterprises.

译文

铁路基本建设应严格按照国家规定的程序进行，必须符合国家相关法律法规，执行国家标准、行业标准和技术规范。设计工作由具有相应资质等级的单位承担，根据已批准的可行性研究报告进行，并充分听取相关铁路企业的意见。

译者注： “单位”不能望文生义译为”unit”，应根据语境选用 “department” 或 “section”。”设计”在句中可用作动词，无需名词化。

原文

第 2A 条

The term “rolling stock” as used in these Regulations includes railway locomotives, passenger coaches, freight wagons, multiple units (EMUs/DMUs), and special self-propelled equipment.

The term “railway enterprise” as used in these Regulations refers to railway transport enterprises and railway asset-owning units.

译文

本规程所称机车车辆包括铁路机车、客车、货车、动车组以及自轮运转特种设备。本规程所称铁路企业是指铁路运输企业以及铁路产权单位。

译者注： “自轮运转”用 “wheel” 体现带轮特征。”transport” 一词全文需统一，保持一致性。

原文

A railway transport enterprise refers to an enterprise legal person engaged in the business of public railway passenger and freight transport. Railway transport enterprises shall obtain a railway transport license according to relevant regulations and conduct railway transport operations within the licensed scope.

译文

铁路运输企业是指从事铁路旅客、货物公共运输营业的企业法人，铁路运输企业按有关规定取得铁路运输许可证，按照许可范围开展铁路运输营业。

译者注： “营业”译为 “service” 或 “business” 更合适，比 “operation” 更贴近运输服务行业的语境。

术语对照： 通知 · notify / inform ·· 建设单位 · (construction project) Owner ·· 施工单位 · construction unit / Contractor ·· 分包商 · sub-contractor

原文

Article 3 Engineering construction shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of the approved design documents, strictly implementing the bidding and supervision systems for construction projects. The relevant railway enterprises shall, in conjunction with the engineering design and construction units, formulate a safe construction plan, construct according to the plan, strengthen environmental protection, and ensure project quality. Upon completion of construction, the site shall be promptly cleaned up without affecting the safety of railway operations.

译文

工程施工按照批准的设计文件的要求进行，严格执行工程建设项目招投标和监理制度。相关铁路企业会同工程设计、施工单位制定安全施工方案，按照方案进行施工，加强环境保护，确保工程质量。施工完毕应及时清理现场，不得影响铁路运营安全。

译者注： 中文原文动词较多（”进行””执行””制定””按照””加强””确保””清理”），英文译文应尽量减少动词，转化为介词短语、分词结构或名词化表达，以符合英文法规文本的严谨简洁风格。

原文

Article 4 Upon completion of new or renovated projects, acceptance checks and safety assessments shall be conducted in accordance with regulations. During track acceptance, the design speed shall be achieved.

The handover can only take place after verifying that the project complies with technical standards and the requirements of the design documents, and confirming that the completion documents and technical equipment manuals are complete. New or renovated engineering facilities shall have a clearly defined warranty period.

译文

新建、改建工程竣工后，应按规定进行验收和安全评估。线路验收时应达到设计速度。在确认工程符合技术标准、设计文件的要求，并检查竣工文件和技术设备使用说明书等资料齐全后，方可交接。新建、改建的工程设施有明确的质量保证期。

译者注： 需强调线路验收与设计速度的关系，不能遗漏”分段验收”（sectional acceptance）的翻译。

第三篇 · 水泥基防水涂料专利

原文

This is a non-provisional application that claims the benefit of U.S. Provisional Application 62/011227 filed 06/12/2014, the entire contents of which are hereby incorporated by reference.

译文

本申请是非临时申请，其主张 2014 年 6 月 12 日申请的美国临时申请 62/011227 的优先权，该临时申请的全部内容在此通过引用并入本文。

译者注： 原文是一个英语长句，一次性表达三层信息：①申请性质（非临时）、②优先权主张、③引用并入声明。译为中文时拆为三个短句，各司其职，既保留全部法律信息，又显著提升了可读性。

原文

The present invention relates to two-component compositions for use in making cementitious waterproofing membranes comprising as component A) one or more acrylic aqueous emulsion copolymer having a measured glass transition temperature (Tg) of from −40 to 0°C, preferably, −20 to 0°C, and comprising the residue of a reducing agent, and, as a separate component B) a fast curing dry mix powder composition of a hydraulic cement and a high alumina content cement.

译文

本发明涉及用于制备水泥基防水涂料的双组分组合物，其包含：作为组分 A）一种或多种丙烯酸水性乳液共聚物，其测量玻璃化转变温度（Tg）为 −40℃ 至 0℃，优选 −20℃ 至 0℃，并且包含还原剂残基；以及作为独立组分 B）的快固化干混粉末组合物，其包含水硬性水泥和高铝水泥。

译者注： “comprising as component A)…… and, as a separate component B)……” 是专利文献的核心逻辑结构，译为”作为组分 A……以及作为独立组分 B”精准传达了双组分物理分离的并列独立关系，这正是专利的灵魂所在。

原文

More particularly, it relates to two component compositions wherein the A) aqueous emulsion copolymer is the copolymerization product of (i) from 60 to 89.9 wt.% of one or more nonionic (meth)acrylic monomers, (ii) from 10 to 40 wt.% of one or more vinyl aromatic monomers, (iii) from 0.1 to 2.0 wt.% of one or more monomers chosen from itaconic acid, methacrylic acid, amides of α,β-unsaturated C3 to C6 carboxylic acids, and mixtures thereof, all wt.%s of monomers based on the total monomer solids, wherein the aqueous emulsion copolymer has at least one residue of an ascorbic acid reducing agent or is the copolymerization product of a monomer (iii) comprising a mixture of itaconic acid and an amide of α,β-unsaturated C3 to C6 carboxylic acid.

译文

更具体地，本发明涉及双组分组合物，其中所述 A）水性乳液共聚物是下述单体的共聚产物：(i) 60 重量% 至 89.9 重量% 的一种或多种非离子性（甲基）丙烯酸酯单体，(ii) 10 重量% 至 40 重量% 的一种或多种乙烯基芳香族单体，(iii) 0.1 重量% 至 2.0 重量% 的一种或多种选自衣康酸、甲基丙烯酸、α,β-不饱和 C3 至 C6 羧酸酰胺及其混合物的单体，所有单体重量百分比均基于单体总固体量，其中所述水性乳液共聚物具有抗坏血酸还原剂的至少一种残基，或者是包含衣康酸和 α,β-不饱和 C3 至 C6 羧酸酰胺的混合物作为单体 (iii) 的共聚产物。

原文

In ordinary Portland cement (OPC) or standard two-component cementitious waterproofing membrane compositions, 2 mortar layers have to be applied to achieve sufficient thickness and waterproofing quality. When using OPC in a dry mix, the time to apply the second layer is after at least 24 hours.

译文

在使用普通硅酸盐水泥（OPC）或标准双组分水泥基防水涂料时，需要涂抹两层砂浆才能达到足够的厚度和防水质量。采用 OPC 干混料时，第二层砂浆的施工需等待至少 24 小时后进行。

原文

Faster curing waterproofing membranes can be achieved by using fast setting cement calcium alumina cement (CAC). However, there are multiple challenges when using emulsion polymers in fast setting cement compositions. As the wet mortar thickens very fast, the applicability and workability become very difficult; pot life is unacceptably short and the resulting waterproofing membrane is too rigid, and thereby lacks flexibility and often cracks so that it is not waterproof.

译文

通过使用快凝高铝水泥（CAC）可以实现快速固化的防水涂料。然而，在快凝水泥组合物中使用乳液聚合物存在多重挑战：由于湿砂浆快速变稠，其涂抹性和可施工性变得非常困难；适用期短得无法接受，且所得防水涂料刚性过高，因此缺乏柔韧性，经常开裂导致无法防水。

译者注： “achieve”不译为”实现”，译为”获得””生产”或”制备”更自然（注意”实现”的宾语应是可以达成的事物，而非产品本身）。”applicability”在此语境下指施工时的涂抹性能，译为”涂抹性”或”施工性”，而非泛泛的”适用性”。”rigid”译为”刚性过高”而非”过于刚性”——“刚性”是形容词，”刚性过高”更标准。此外，快凝带来的另一个问题是”挠性”（flexibility）不足，这是防水涂料的关键技术指标，应使用专业术语”挠性”而非”柔韧性”。

原文

Further, in fast setting waterproofing membrane compositions, the emulsion polymer does not provide enough flexibility to give sufficient crack bridging in dry/wet conditions.

译文

此外，在快凝防水涂料组合物中，乳液聚合物无法提供足够的挠性以满足干/湿条件下的充分裂纹桥接能力。

原文

One very expensive way to solve the problem of the rigidity of the resulting waterproofing membrane would be to reduce the glass transition temperature (Tg) of the emulsion polymer, making it softer and more flexible, and to increase significantly the polymer to cement ratio.

译文

解决所得防水涂料刚性问题的一种成本非常高的方法是降低乳液聚合物的玻璃化转变温度（Tg）使其更软、更挠性，并显著提高聚合物与水泥的比例。
