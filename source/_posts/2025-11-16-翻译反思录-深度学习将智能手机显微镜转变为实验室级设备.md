## 第一篇：深度学习将智能手机显微镜转变为实验室级别设备

**原文**

Deep learning transforms smartphone microscopes into laboratory-grade devices

**初译**

深度学习将智能手机显微镜转变（爆改）为实验室级别设备

> **译者注**

> 本文为新闻。新闻可以吸引人眼球。夸张一些。转变可以改为别的词语比如爆改

---

By Amy Akmal

**原文**

1 Researchers at the UCLA Samueli School of Engineering have demonstrated that deep learning, a powerful form of artificial intelligence, can discern and enhance microscopic details in photos taken by smartphones. The technique improves the resolution and color details of smartphone images so much that they approach the quality of images from laboratory-grade microscopes.

**译文**

加州大学洛杉矶分校萨穆埃利工程学院的研究人员证明，强大的人工智能技术——深度学习能够识别并增强智能手机拍摄照片中的微观细节。该技术显著提升了智能手机图像的分辨率和色彩细节，使其画质可媲美实验室级别的显微镜图像。

> **译者注**

> enhance改进。使微观细节更清晰。

---

**原文**

2 The advance could help bring high-quality medical diagnostics into resource-poor regions, where people otherwise do not have access to high-end diagnostic technologies. And the technique uses attachments that can be inexpensively produced with a 3-D printer, at less than $100 a piece, versus the thousands of dollars it would cost to buy laboratory-grade equipment that produces images of similar quality.

**译文**

这一进展有助于在资源匮乏地区实现高质量医疗诊断，这些地区原本无法获得高端诊断技术。该技术所使用的附加装置可通过3D打印机廉价生产，单价低于100美元，而购买能产生同等画质图像的实验室级设备则需花费数千美元。

---

**原文**

3 Cameras on today's smartphones are designed to photograph people and scenery, not to produce high-resolution microscopic images. So the researchers developed an attachment that can be placed over the smartphone lens to increase the resolution and the visibility of tiny details of the images they take, down to a scale of approximately one millionth of a meter.

**译文**

当今智能手机的摄像头是为拍摄人物和风景而设计，并非用于生成高分辨率显微图像。为此研究人员开发了一种可加装于手机镜头上的附加装置，能将拍摄图像的解析度分辨率提升至约百万分之一米级别，增强微小细节的可见性。

> **译者注**

> 解析度改为分辨率。

---

**原文**

4 But that only solved part of the challenge, because no attachment would be enough to compensate for the difference in quality between smartphone cameras' image sensors and lenses and those of high-end lab equipment. The new technique compensates for the difference by using artificial intelligence to reproduce the level of resolution and color details needed for a laboratory analysis.

**译文**

但这仅解决了部分难题，因为任何附加装置都无法完全弥补智能手机摄像头图像传感器／镜头与高端实验室设备之间的质量差距。新技术通过人工智能生成（重现/再现）实验室分析所需的分辨率与色彩细节水平，成功弥补了这一差距。

> **译者注**

> reproduce可以翻为重现或者再现。不要生成。

---

**原文**

5 The research was led by Aydogan Ozcan, Chancellor's Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Bioengineering, and Yair Rivenson, a UCLA postdoctoral scholar. Ozcan's research group has introduced several innovations in mobile microscopy and sensing, and it maintains a particular focus on developing field-portable medical diagnostics and sensors for resource-poor areas.

**译文**

本研究由电子与计算机工程及生物工程系特聘教授艾多甘·奥兹詹和博士后研究员亚伊尔·里文松共同领导。奥兹詹研究团队已在移动显微技术和传感领域推出多项创新成果，特别专注于为资源匮乏地区开发便携式医疗诊断设备与传感器。

---

**原文**

6 "Using deep learning, we set out to bridge the gap in image quality between inexpensive mobile phone-based microscopes and gold-standard bench-top microscopes that use high-end lenses," Ozcan said.

**初译**

奥兹詹表示："我们运用深度学习技术，旨在弥补基于廉价手机的显微镜与使用高端镜头的黄金标准台式显微镜之间的图像质量差距。"

> **译者注**

> 弥合差距的搭配比弥补要好。

**终译**

奥兹詹表示："我们运用深度学习技术，旨在弥合基于廉价手机的显微镜与使用高端镜头的黄金标准台式显微镜之间的图像质量差距。"

---

**原文**

7 "We believe that our approach is broadly applicable to other low-cost microscopy systems that use, for example, inexpensive lenses or cameras, and could facilitate the replacement of high-end bench-top microscopes with cost-effective, mobile alternatives."

**初译**

"我们相信该方法可广泛适用于其他低成本显微镜系统，例如使用廉价镜头或摄像头的设备，有望推动高端台式显微镜被具有成本效益的移动设备替代。"

> **译者注**

**终译**

"我们相信该方法可广泛适用于其他低成本显微镜系统，例如使用廉价镜头或摄像头的设备，有助于用经济高效的移动方案取代高端台式显微镜。"

**原文**

8 He added that the new technique could find numerous applications in global health, telemedicine and diagnostics-related applications.

**初译**

他补充说，这项新技术可在全球健康、远程医疗和诊断相关应用领域找到广泛用途。

> **译者注**

**终译**

他补充说，这项新技术有望在全球健康、远程医疗和诊断相关领域获得广泛应用。

---

**原文**

9 The researchers shot images of lung tissue samples, blood and Pap smears, first using a standard laboratory-grade microscope, and then with a smartphone with the 3D-printed microscope attachment. The researchers then fed the pairs of corresponding images into a computer system that "learns" how to rapidly enhance the mobile phone images. The process relies on a deep-learning-based computer code, which was developed by the UCLA researchers.

**译文**

研究团队先后使用标准实验室级显微镜和加装3D打印显微镜附件的智能手机，对肺组织样本、血液和巴氏涂片进行拍摄。随后将两组对应图像输入计算机系统，训练其"学会"如何快速增强手机图像质量。该过程依托于加州大学洛杉矶分校研究人员开发的基于深度学习的计算机代码。

---

**原文**

10 To see if their technique would work on other types of lower-quality images, the researchers used deep learning to successfully perform similar transformations with images that had lost some detail because they were compressed for either faster transmission over a computer network or more efficient storage.

**译文**

为验证技术是否适用于其他类型的低画质图像，研究人员对因网络传输或存储压缩而丢失细节的图像进行测试，成功通过深度学习实现了画质提升。

---

**原文**

11 The study was published in ACS Photonics, a journal of the American Chemical Society. It builds upon previous studies by Ozcan's group that used deep learning to reconstruct holograms and improve microscopy.

**译文**

这项研究发表于美国化学会旗下期刊《ACS 光子学》，是在奥兹詹团队先前利用深度学习重建全息图及改进显微技术研究基础上的新突破。
