## 1. 中国海油与道达尔签署液化天然气合作协议

**原文**

当地时间3月26日，中国海洋石油总公司与法国道达尔公司在法国巴黎签署了《液化天然气合作协议》。

**译文**

CNOOC and Total Sign LNG Cooperation Agreement. On March 26 local time, China National Offshore Oil Corporation (CNOOC) and France's Total signed an LNG cooperation agreement in Paris, France.

---

**原文**

中国国家主席习近平和法国总统奥朗德出席了签字仪式。

**译文**

The signing ceremony was attended by Chinese President Xi Jinping and French President François Hollande.

---

**原文**

这份协议是中法两国之间此次签署的系列协议之一。

**译文**

The agreement is one of a series of deals signed between China and France during this occasion.

---

**原文**

中国海油和道达尔希望在中国天然气市场再增加每年100万吨长期LNG资源供应，以及加强在LNG产业链的全面合作。

**译文**

CNOOC and Total aim to further expand their cooperation by adding an annual supply of 1 million tons of long-term LNG resources to the Chinese natural gas market, while also strengthening comprehensive collaboration across the LNG industry chain.

---

**原文**

自2008年中国海油与道达尔签署每年100万吨长期LNG资源购销协议以来，双方合作良好。

**译文**

Since the two companies signed a long-term LNG sales and purchase agreement for 1 million tons per year in 2008, their partnership has progressed smoothly.

---

**原文**

签署并执行了一系列中短期和现货LNG协议。

**译文**

They have since signed and executed a series of medium- and short-term LNG agreements, as well as spot LNG deals.

---

**原文**

截至目前，道达尔已向中国海油交付了总量超过500万吨的LNG资源。

**译文**

To date, Total has delivered a total of over 5 million tons of LNG resources to CNOOC.

## 2. 中国海油发明专利授权量首进国内企业前十

**原文**

3月24日，从中国海洋石油总公司科技发展部获悉。

**译文**

CNOOC Ranks Among Top 10 Domestic Enterprises in Invention Patent Grants. On March 24, it was learned from CNOOC's Science and Technology Development Department.

---

**原文**

在国家知识产权局近日公布的2013年国内企业（不含港澳台）发明专利授权量排行中，中国海油以275项发明专利授权量首次进入国内企业发明专利授权量前十位，位列第十。

**译文**

According to the recent ranking of invention patent grants for domestic enterprises (not including Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan) in 2013 released by the National Intellectual Property Administration, CNOOC entered the top 10 list for the first time, ranking tenth with 275 invention patents granted.

> **译者注**
> 
> not include的使用更合适，exclude有排除在外的意思偏贬义不合适。
> 
---

**原文**

2013年，总公司共获得国内专利授权708项，其中，发明专利275项。

**译文**

In 2013, the corporation was granted a total of 708 domestic patents, of which 275 were invention patents.

---

**原文**

发明专利主要集中在上游板块，主要来源于研究总院、中海油服、海油发展和海油工程等单位。

**译文**

The invention patents were primarily concentrated in the upstream sector, mainly originating from entities such as the Research Institute, COSL, CNOOC Energy Technology & Services Limited, and COOEC.

> **译者注**
> 
> 来源太重。
> 
---

**原文**

在前十位的榜单上，华为技术有限公司以2251项的发明专利授权量位居首位。

**译文**

On the top 10 list, Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. ranked first with 2,251 invention patents granted.

---

**原文**

中国石化和中国石油以1627项和527项的发明专利授权量分列第二位和第四位。

**译文**

Sinopec and CNPC ranked second and fourth with 1,627 and 527 invention patents granted, respectively.

---

**原文**

中国工程院院士周守为在同期举办的以"技术革新与新能源"为主题的第六届国际石油产业高峰论坛上致辞。

**译文**

> 
> 三天可以放在前面更好。
>

**译文**

**原文**

**原文**

**译文**

Newly developed offshore equipment products from domestic and international companies, such as marine blowout preventers, subsea Christmas trees, offshore drilling riser systems, and high-pressure metering pumps designed to meet deepwater oil and gas development needs, were unveiled at the exhibition.
