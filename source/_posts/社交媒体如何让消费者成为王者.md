**原文**

How Social Media has Made the Consumer King

Information is power. Quite simply, those who control information have power over others. For millennia, power was in the hands of the few—those individuals in society who controlled both the flow of information and peoples' access to it. Today, we are in the midst of a revolution; a revolution brought on by the power of information-sharing. Today, no one person or group has a monopoly on information; information is owned by everyone. What has this meant for businesses? It has meant that they have had to start listening to consumers like never before.

In the past, consumers learned about products and services when marketers and advertisers created messages that were then pushed through traditional mass-media channels. Information, in other words, was one-directional; the message was fully controlled by the brand. Thanks to the emergence of user-generated content and social media, businesses are no longer in complete control. The consumer has acquired immense power. Their voice is now everywhere. In 2020 alone, UK consumers left well over 200 million comments online. The power of digital opinion is now mightier than the power of the advertising dollar.

Social media has changed the way people do business and influenced the entire buying process. Consumers now use social media outlets to research companies before doing business with them. According to one study, two thirds of consumers use information they find through social media to influence their buying decisions. Roughly the same percentage (67%) are then likely to pass this information on to others and more than 60% trust the information they find through social media more than they do via traditional advertisements. Customers can get feedback and price comparisons at the point of purchase, in real time, through applications, sites or postings in social networks. With consumers offering other consumers product reviews and suggestions, consumers now have the upper hand. Social media has also made consumers feel empowered to say the things they've always wanted to say. In one study, 20% of U.S. social media users said that they had lashed out against brands and companies online. Social media has also given rise to the "critical consumer": consumers who are so well versed in the marketplace that they are increasingly hard to persuade. One study revealed that over half (56%) of consumers check two to three websites before purchasing an item, with 21% checking more than three websites. As disposable income is squeezed, impulsive shopping is fast becoming a thing of the past. Today's consumer is an "information shopper", someone who will only buy after conducting research online. Three quarters of those surveyed said they will wait "as long as it takes" to secure the best deal. And today's consumer does not stop once their purchase has been made. 40% of those surveyed stated that they continued to monitor the product they had just bought to ensure they got value for money.

Today having a presence on social media is an important part of any company's marketing strategy. Companies have been forced to build a strong presence in social media sites in order to react quickly to negative feedback and customers' demands; to develop a relationship with their customers; to influence the direction of the conversation; and, ultimately, to make contact with their customers.

**译文**

社交媒体如何让消费者成为王者

信息即权力。简而言之，掌控信息的人拥有凌驾他人之上的力量。数千年来，权力始终掌握在少数人手中——那些既能控制信息流向又垄断信息获取渠道的社会个体。今天，我们正身处一场革命之中：这场革命由信息共享的力量所催生。当下，没有任何个人或组织能垄断信息；信息为全民共有。这对企业意味着什么？意味着他们必须以前所未有的姿态倾听消费者的声音。

过去，消费者通过营销人员和广告商精心制作、经传统大众媒体渠道推送的信息来了解产品与服务。换言之，信息是单向流动的，品牌方完全掌控着话语权。随着用户生成内容和社交媒体的兴起，企业再也无法完全操控局面。消费者获得了巨大的权力，他们的声音无处不在。仅2020年，英国消费者就在线上留下了超过两亿条评论。数字舆论的力量已然超越了广告投放的效果。

社交媒体改变了商业运作模式，并深刻影响了整个购买流程。如今的消费者在与企业打交道之前，会借助社交媒体渠道对其进行调研。一项研究表明，三分之二的消费者会利用从社交媒体获取的信息来左右其购买决策。约同等比例（67%）的受访者随后会将此类信息传递给他人，超过60%的人认为社交媒体信息的可信度超越了传统广告。消费者可通过应用程序、网站或社交网络动态，在购买时实时获取产品反馈和比价信息。当消费者之间互相提供产品评价与建议时，权力已然转移到了消费者手中。社交媒体还让消费者敢于说出自己一直想说的话。在一项研究中，20%的美国社交媒体用户表示曾在线上抨击过品牌与企业。社交媒体还催生了"批判型消费者"——这类人群深谙市场规则，越来越难以被说服。研究显示，超过半数（56%）的消费者在购买商品前会查看两到三个网站进行比价，21%的人甚至会查看超过三个网站。随着可支配收入的收紧，冲动消费正迅速成为历史。今天的消费者是"信息型购物者"，只有经过在线调研后才会出手购买。四分之三的受访者表示，他们会"不惜等待多久"来锁定最优价格。而今天的消费者在完成购买之后并不会止步：40%的人表示会持续追踪刚刚购买的产品，确保物有所值。

如今，在社交媒体上建立存在已成为企业营销战略的重要环节。企业被迫在社交媒体平台上构建强大的存在感，以快速应对负面反馈和客户需求，建立客户关系，引导对话方向，并最终实现与客户的深度连接。
