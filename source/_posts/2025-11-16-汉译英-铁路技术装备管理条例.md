## 第二篇：铁路技术装备管理条例

### 第一编 技术设备 Part I Technical Equipment

### 第一章 基本要求 Chapter I Basic Requirements

#### 基建、制造及其验收交接 Capital Construction, Manufacturing, and Acceptance Handover

**原文**

第1条 铁路的基本建设、产品制造应综合配套，保证质量，采用保证行车安全的技术装备，不断提高运输能力，以适应国民经济发展需要。

**译文**

Railway capital construction and product manufacturing shall be comprehensively coordinated, quality assured, and equipped with technology that ensures operational safety. The goal is to continuously enhance transport capacity to meet the needs of national economic development.

**原文**

应采用安全可靠、先进成熟、经济适用的技术，实现技术装备标准化、系列化、模块化、信息化，加快实现铁路现代化。

**译文**

Safe, reliable, advanced, mature, economically viable, and applicable technologies shall be adopted to achieve standardization, serialization, modularization, and informatization of technical equipment, thereby accelerating the modernization of railways.

---

**原文**

第2条 铁路基本建设应严格按照国家规定的程序进行，必须符合国家相关法律法规，执行国家标准、行业标准和技术规范。

**译文**

Railway capital construction shall strictly adhere to the procedures stipulated by the state, comply with relevant national laws and regulations, and implement national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications.

**原文**

设计工作应由具有相应资质等级的单位承担，根据已批准的可行性研究报告进行，并充分听取相关铁路企业的意见。

Design work shall be undertaken by units with corresponding qualification grades, based on the approved feasibility study report, and shall fully solicit opinions from relevant railway enterprises.

**原文**

**译文**

**原文**

第3条 工程施工应按照批准的设计文件的要求进行，严格执行工程建设项目招投标和监理制度。相关铁路企业应会同工程设计、施工单位制定安全施工方案，按照方案进行施工，加强环境保护，确保工程质量。施工完毕应及时清理现场，不得影响铁路运营安全。

**译文**

Article 3 Engineering construction shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of the approved design documents, strictly implementing the bidding and supervision systems for construction projects. The relevant railway enterprises shall, in conjunction with the engineering design and construction units, formulate a safe construction plan, construct according to the plan, strengthen environmental protection, and ensure project quality.

> **译者注**
> 
> 动词较多，可以尽量减少动词。

> Upon completion of construction, the site shall be promptly cleaned up without affecting the safety of railway operations.
> 
**译文**
> 
> 施工完毕应及时清理现场，不得影响铁路运营安全。
> 
**原文**
> 
> 涉及营业线施工时，应按铁路企业规定程序审批，且应保证行车安全，减少对运输的影响。
> 
**译文**
> 
> When construction involves existing railway lines, it shall be approved according to the procedures stipulated by the railway enterprise, ensuring traffic safety and minimizing the impact on transportation.
>

第4条 新建、改建工程竣工后，应按规定进行验收和安全评估。线路验收时应达到设计速度。

**译文**

Article 4 Upon completion of new or renovated projects, acceptance checks and safety assessments shall be conducted in accordance with regulations. During track acceptance, the design speed shall be achieved.

**原文**

第5条 铁路基本建设项目中的环境保护、节能、水土保持、劳动安全、职业卫生、消防、安全防护、公共安全等设施，应和主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产。

**译文**

Article 5 Environmental protection, energy conservation, soil and water conservation, labor safety, occupational health, fire protection, safety precautions, public safety, and other facilities within railway capital construction projects shall be designed, constructed, and put into operation simultaneously with the main project.

> **译者注**
> 
> 公共安全（和国家安全是一个层面，security有人蓄意/谋划破坏的意思）。
>

**原文**

第6条 铁路产品应符合国家和铁道行业技术标准。铁路重要产品应按有关规定，执行行政许可、产品认证等铁路产品准入制度。

**译文**

Article 6 Railway products shall comply with national and railway industry technical standards. Important railway products shall implement railway product access systems, such as administrative licensing and product certification, in accordance with relevant regulations.

---

**原文**

第7条 铁路企业应严格控制进入铁路的产品质量，建立必要的产品质量检验和质量问题追究制度。质量抽查不合格或实行准入管理但未获得相关资质的产品，不得在铁路使用。

**译文**

Article 7 Railway enterprises shall strictly control the quality of products entering the railway sector and establish necessary product quality inspection and quality issue tracking systems. Products that fail quality spot checks or that are subject to access management but lack the required qualifications shall not be used on the railway.

**原文**

制造、检修的机车车辆及其重要配件经铁路企业或其委托的专业机构监造，符合要求后，方准交付使用。

**译文**

Manufactured or overhauled locomotives, rolling stock, and their important components can only be delivered for use after being inspected by the railway enterprise or a professional institution authorized by it and confirmed to meet requirements.

> **译者注**
> 
> 先后顺序应该先让后方的xx去监造。
> 
---

**原文**

第8条 新设备（包括改造后的设备）投入使用前应有操作规程、竣工图纸等技术文件和保证安全生产的办法与管理细则，经过技术测验合格并对有关人员进行培训后，方可使用。

**译文**

Article 8 Before new equipment (including modified equipment) is put into use, there shall be operational procedures, as-built drawings, and other technical documents, as well as measures and management rules to ensure safety. It can only be used after passing technical tests and the completion of training for relevant personnel.

---

**原文**

第9条 铁路机车车辆、线路、桥隧、通信、信号、牵引供电、电力、信息、安全、给水、房建等技术设备，均应有完整和正确反映其技术状态的文件及技术履历等有关资料。

**译文**

Article 9 Technical equipment such as railway locomotives and rolling stock, tracks, bridges and tunnels, communications, signaling, traction power supply, power systems, information systems, safety systems, water supply, and buildings shall have complete and accurate documentation reflecting their technical status, including technical histories and other relevant data.

**原文**

上述技术资料由有关部门或单位妥善保管，并根据变化情况及时记载修订。

**译文**

The relevant departments or units shall properly keep the aforementioned technical documentation and update it promptly based on changes.

---

**原文**

第10条 机车车辆等技术设备应有行业统一规定的标记。隐蔽的建（构）筑物及设备应在地面上设有标志。

**译文**

Article 10 Technical equipment such as locomotives and rolling stock shall bear the uniformly prescribed markings within the industry. Concealed structures (or buildings) and equipment shall have above-ground markers.

---

**原文**

第11条 铁路企业应制定机车、客车、货车、动车组等主要设备的资产技术管理制度。机车车辆重大技术改变等涉及行政许可管理的事项，应按规定办理相应的行政许可手续。

**译文**

Article 11 Railway enterprises shall establish asset technical management systems for major equipment such as locomotives, passenger coaches, freight wagons, and multiple units. For significant technical modifications to locomotives and rolling stock involving administrative licensing management, the corresponding administrative licensing procedures shall be completed in accordance with regulations.

---

**原文**

第12条 高原铁路应考虑高寒缺氧特点，为旅客和工作人员提供必要的救助设施，应采用高可靠、少维修、远程监控的运输设备。

**译文**

Article 12 Plateau railways shall take into account the characteristics of high altitude, cold temperatures, and hypoxia, providing necessary rescue facilities for passengers and staff. Transportation equipment with high reliability, low maintenance requirements, and remote monitoring capabilities shall be adopted.

---

**原文**

第13条 对现有不符合本规程规定标准的技术设备，有条件的应逐步改造或更换，未改造完之前铁路企业应制定安全措施。

**译文**

Article 13 For existing technical equipment that does not comply with the standards specified in these Regulations, gradual upgrades or replacements shall be carried out where conditions permit. Prior to the completion of such upgrades, railway enterprises shall formulate safety measures.
