## 原文与译文



**原文**

For geneticists, autism is a vexing challenge. Inheritance patterns suggest it has a strong genetic component. But variants in scores of genes known to play some role in autism can explain only about 20% of all cases. Finding other variants that might contribute requires looking for clues in data on the 25,000 other human genes and their surrounding DNA—an overwhelming task for human investigators. So computational biologist Olga Troyanskaya of Princeton University and the Simons Foundation in New York City enlisted the tools of artificial intelligence (AI). "We can only do so much as biologists to show what underlies diseases like autism," explains collaborator Robert Darnell. "The power of machines to ask a trillion questions where a scientist can ask just 10 is a game-changer." Darnell is the founding director of the New York Genome Center and a physician scientist at The Rockefeller University in New York City.

Troyanskaya combined hundreds of data sets on which genes are active in specific human cells, how proteins interact, and where transcription factor binding sites and other key genome features are located. Then her team used machine learning to build a map of gene interactions and compared those of the few well-established autism risk genes with those of thousands of other unknown genes, looking for similarities. That flagged another 2500 genes likely to be involved in autism, they reported last year in Nature Neuroscience. But genes don't act in isolation, as geneticists have recently realized. Their behavior is shaped by the millions of nearby noncoding bases, which interact with DNA-binding proteins and other factors. Identifying which noncoding variants might affect nearby autism genes is an even tougher problem than finding the genes in the first place, and graduate student Jian Zhou in Troyanskaya's Princeton lab is deploying AI to solve it.

To train the program—a deep-learning system—Zhou exposed it to data collected by the Encyclopedia of DNA Elements and Roadmap Epigenomics. These are two projects that cataloged how tens of thousands of noncoding DNA sites affect neighboring genes. The system in effect learned which features to look for as it evaluates unknown stretches of noncoding DNA for potential activity. When Zhou and Troyanskaya described their program, called DeepSEA, in Nature Methods in October 2015, Xiaohui Xie, a computer scientist at the University of California, Irvine, called it "a milestone in applying deep learning to genomics." Now, the Princeton team is running the genomes of autism patients through DeepSEA, hoping to rank the impacts of noncoding bases. Xie is also applying AI to the genome, though with a broader focus than autism. He, too, hopes to classify any mutations by the odds they are harmful. But he cautions that in genomics, deep learning systems are only as good as the data sets on which they are trained. "Right now I think people are skeptical" that such systems can reliably parse the genome, he says. "But I think down the road more and more people will embrace deep learning."

**译文**

对遗传学家而言，自闭症是一个棘手的挑战。其遗传模式表明它具有很强的遗传成分。但已知在自闭症中起一定作用的众多基因变异，只能解释约20%的病例。要寻找其他可能起作用的变异，就需要从另外25000个人类基因及其周围DNA的数据中寻找线索——这对人类研究者来说是一项艰巨的任务。因此，普林斯顿大学和纽约西蒙斯基金会的计算生物学家奥尔加·特罗扬斯卡娅求助于人工智能工具。合作者罗伯特·达内尔解释道："我们在揭示像自闭症这类疾病的根本原因方面能力有限。而机器能提出一万亿个问题，科学家只能提出十个，这彻底改变了游戏规则。"达内尔是纽约基因组中心的创始主任，也是纽约市洛克菲勒大学的一名医师科学家。

特罗扬斯卡娅整合了数百个数据集，内容涉及特定人类细胞中哪些基因是活跃的、蛋白质如何相互作用，以及转录因子结合位点和其他关键基因组特征位于何处。然后，她的团队利用机器学习构建了一个基因相互作用图谱，并将少数几个已明确的自闭症风险基因与数千个其他未知基因的相互作用进行比较，寻找相似之处。他们在去年发表于《自然-神经科学》的报告中指出，这又标记出2500个可能涉及自闭症的基因。但正如遗传学家最近意识到的，基因并非孤立发挥作用。它们的行为受到附近数以百万计非编码碱基的影响，这些碱基与DNA结合蛋白及其他因子相互作用。确定哪些非编码变异可能影响附近的自闭症基因，甚至比最初寻找这些基因更为棘手，特罗扬斯卡娅在普林斯顿实验室的研究生周健正在运用人工智能来解决这个问题。

为了训练这个程序（一个深度学习系统），周健用"DNA元件百科全书"和"路线图表观基因组学计划"收集的数据对其进行训练。这两大项目系统地列出了数万个非编码DNA位点如何影响邻近基因。该系统在评估未知非编码DNA片段的潜在活性时，本质上学会了需要寻找哪些特征。当周健和特罗扬斯卡娅在2015年10月的《自然-方法学》上描述他们名为DeepSEA的程序时，加州大学欧文分校的计算机科学家谢晓辉称之为"将深度学习应用于基因组学的一个里程碑"。如今，普林斯顿的这个研究小组正通过DeepSEA分析自闭症患者的基因组，希望能对非编码碱基的影响程度进行排序。谢晓辉也在将人工智能应用于基因组，但其关注点比自闭症更广泛。他也希望根据突变可能有害的概率对其进行分类。但他提醒说，在基因组学中，深度学习系统的效能完全取决于其训练所用的数据集。"目前我认为人们对此类系统能否可靠解析基因组持怀疑态度，"他说，"但我认为，未来将有越来越多人接受深度学习。"

> **译者注：**
>
> "as biologists" 并非指"作为生物学家"，而是"以生物学家的能力所能做到的极限"，暗含机器能力远超人类的对比。
> "a game-changer" 译为"彻底改变了游戏规则"，兼顾口语化和冲击力。
> "exposed it to data" 中的 "exposed to" 译为"用……对其进行训练"，比直译"暴露给"更符合机器学习领域的表达习惯。
> "only as good as" 关注的是系统效能而非好坏，译为"效能完全取决于"更准确。
> "be shaped by" 译为"受到……的影响"，而非"被……塑造"，在生物学语境中更自然。
