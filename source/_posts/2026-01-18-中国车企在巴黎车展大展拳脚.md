## 翻译正文

**原文**

Chinese Automakers Display Force at Paris Auto Show

**译文**

中国车企在巴黎车展展现强大实力

---

**原文**

By Melissa Eddy and Liz Alderman

**译文**

作者：梅丽莎·埃迪 & 莉兹·奥尔德曼

---

**原文**

There is no mistaking the signal sent by the phalanx of Chinese automakers at the Paris auto show this week. They are coming to Europe — new tariffs be damned.

**译文**

本周巴黎车展上，中国汽车制造商的密集亮相传递了一个明确无误的信号：他们正在进入欧洲——全然不顾新的关税壁垒。

---

**原文**

BYD, the country's largest electric vehicle maker, and eight other Chinese automakers have taken a prominent position at the Mondial de l'Auto, a showcase for the latest in car design, technology and innovation, as they seek to expand market share abroad.

**译文**

中国最大的电动汽车制造商比亚迪和其他八家中国车企，在巴黎车展——这一汽车设计、技术和创新的展示窗口——占据了显眼位置，力图拓展海外市场份额。

---

**原文**

Chinese brands are using the event to show the competition they can make. "It is a window of opportunity to get new customers and more business," François Zhang, deputy general manager of BYD in France, said at the show, held in a cavernous convention hall in southern Paris.

**译文**

中国品牌正利用这次展会展示他们的竞争实力。比亚迪法国公司副总经理张富渊在展会现场表示："这是赢得新客户和更多业务的窗口期。"本届车展在巴黎南部一座巨大的会展中心举办。

---

**原文**

The automakers from China are attending the biennial global event, which opened Monday, as one of several strategies considered among Chinese companies to try to expand their presence in Europe, Mr. Zhang said.

**译文**

张先生表示，中国车企参加这个周一开始的两年一届的全球盛会，是中国企业尝试扩大欧洲市场版图的策略之一。

---

**原文**

The Xi'an-based company also plans to open a dealership in France and five more in other European countries next year, he said.

**译文**

他还透露，这家总部位于西安的公司计划明年在法国开设一个经销店，并在其他五个欧洲国家开设更多经销店。

---

**原文**

At the show, BYD boasted five vehicles, including the Yangwang U8, a sport utility vehicle priced from 152,000 euros (about $166,000) that can float in water and turn around on the spot.

**译文**

比亚迪在本届车展上自豪地亮出了五款车型，其中包括仰望U8——一款起售价为15.2万欧元（约合16.6万美元）的运动型多用途车，具备浮水行驶和原地掉头功能。

---

**原文**

"Our competitors are not so much the other Chinese brands, but the European brands," he said. "We are trying to get customers from them rather than our compatriots."

**译文**

"我们的竞争对手与其说是其他中国品牌，不如说是欧洲品牌，"他说，"我们试图从他们那里争取客户，而非从同胞手中抢客户。"

---

**原文**

Chinese automakers have been on a tear in China, the world's largest car market, where government subsidies and the rapid growth of EV sales have supercharged the industry. But the home market is becoming increasingly saturated, pushing Chinese automakers to seek buyers abroad.

**译文**

中国车企在全球最大的汽车市场——中国国内市场上一路高歌猛进，政府补贴和电动汽车销售的快速增长为该行业注入了强劲动力。但国内市场的竞争日趋白热化，推动着中国车企向海外寻找买家。

---

**原文**

They have been gaining ground in Southeast Asia and South America, but have recently set their sights on Europe. The expansion has been turbocharged by Beijing's push to dominate the global market for EVs, as Chinese executives worry about a glut of cars at home.

**译文**

他们已在东南亚和南美洲站稳脚跟，但最近将目光投向了欧洲。中国领导层推动主导全球电动汽车市场的战略为这一扩张提供了强大动力，与此同时，中国企业高管也在担忧国内市场的产能过剩。

---

**原文**

The arrival of Chinese automakers in Europe is expected to intensify competition in a market where mainstream European brands like Renault and Volkswagen are scrambling to develop affordable EVs. Already, Chinese automakers account for 3 percent of the European car market, and that share could rise to 5 percent in the next several years, according to the Paris-based consulting firm Acteam.

**译文**

中国车企登陆欧洲预计将加剧市场竞争——在此市场中，雷诺和大众等欧洲主流品牌正争相开发价格亲民的电动汽车。根据巴黎咨询公司Acteam的数据，中国车企目前在欧洲汽车市场占有3%的份额，未来几年可能增至5%。

---

**原文**

The growing Chinese presence comes as the European Union's new tariffs of up to 45 percent on electric vehicles from China went into effect in July. The European Commission acknowledged that Chinese EVs were "benefitting from unfair subsidization," and said the tariffs were needed to level the playing field.

**译文**

日益增长的中国车企存在感正值欧盟对中国电动汽车征收高达45%的新关税于7月生效之际。欧盟委员会坦言中国电动汽车"因不公平补贴而获益"，并称关税是为了创造公平竞争环境。

---

**原文**

But Chinese automakers said such tariffs would not stop them. BYD has been able to keep prices low and competitive because it makes most of its components, including its own batteries. BYD's competitor SAIC, a state-owned Chinese company, undercut most rivals by selling the compact MG3 hybrid at around €20,000.

**译文**

但中国车企表示此类关税不会阻止他们。比亚迪之所以能将价格压低并保持竞争力，是因为其大部分零部件——包括电池——均为自产。比亚迪的竞争对手上汽集团——一家中国国有企业——以约两万欧元的价格销售紧凑型MG3混合动力车，低于大多数竞争对手。

---

**原文**

"It is the Chinese who are the most competitive on electrification in the world," the French finance minister, Antoine Armand, told reporters at the show. "We must protect our own industrial and commercial fabric, but we must also work with those who can help us move forward."

**译文**

法国财政部长安托万·阿尔芒在展会上对记者说："在电动化领域，中国是世界上最具竞争力的。我们必须保护自己的工商业结构，但也必须与那些能帮助我们前进的伙伴合作。"

---

**原文**

The Chinese government's subsidies have enabled its automakers to innovate and gain a considerable head start in EV battery technology. And despite European tariffs, the price advantage of Chinese cars over less generously subsidized European and American cars is simply too great for many consumers to bypass, analysts said.

**译文**

中国政府的补贴使其汽车制造商得以创新，并在电动汽车电池技术方面取得了显著的先发优势。分析人士表示，尽管欧洲征收了关税，但中国汽车相比补贴较少的欧美汽车的价格优势实在太大，对许多消费者而言是不可抗拒的。
