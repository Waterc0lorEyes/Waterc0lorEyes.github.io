王者荣耀：不止是游戏

2025-02-03 · 翻译作业

当大多数人把《王者荣耀》仅仅看作一款游戏时，它的影响远不止于此。从宿舍里的深夜五排到办公室午休的快速对局，从传统文化的数字化新生到玩家用AI创作的英雄歌曲——这款游戏已经成为一代人的共同语言。

Honor of Kings: More Than Just a Game

Good morning everyone! Today I want to talk about a game we all love — Honor of Kings.

When we talk about Honor of Kings, most people see it as just a game. But in reality, its impact runs much deeper.

社交：虚拟战场的现实联结

The popularity of Honor of Kings doesn’t just stem from its gameplay, but from how it has reshaped the way young people socialize and express themselves. In this game, “team battles” are no longer just a gaming term—they’ve become a social necessity. Late-night five-player matches in dorm rooms, quick lunchtime games among office workers, even strangers bonding over “CPDD” — all of these prove one thing: Honor of Kings has helped solve the problem of real-world socialization through a virtual battlefield.

文化：跨越虚实边界的桥梁

But Honor of Kings is more than a social tool—it’s a culture bridge between worlds. When we set aside our biases, we see that Honor of Kings has long surpassed the boundaries of entertainment, becoming a unique bridge between the real and the virtual. Beyond the game itself, these characters are more than pixels—they are legendary figures with their own backgrounds and stories.

情感：当英雄开口唱歌

And nothing captures this emotional connection better than the players’ own creations. What left a deep impression on me and still remains in my mind is a song called “天若有情”. This song was created by players using AI technology. It was an AI-generated song featuring the voices of over a dozen female heroes from Honor of Kings. Some of the lyrics in the song even metaphorically correspond to the historical stories of the heroes, making the players feel as if the heroes were like real people right beside them. After countless players heard the songs sung by their favorite heroes, they couldn’t help but be moved to tears.

发问与升华

So when we ask, “Is Honor of Kings just a game?” — the answer is clear. Honor of Kings has gone far beyond being a “pastime” — it is a digital cafe for youth socialization, a new stage for traditional culture to break into the mainstream, and a virtual arena where ordinary people can showcase their creativity. While some still debate whether gaming is a “waste of time,” perhaps we should ask a more meaningful question: Why has this game become the common language of a generation?

When future historians look back on our era, they may not record individual matches or specific heroes. But they will surely note this: There was once a game that blurred the lines between the virtual and the real, that made loneliness and connection coexist, and that brought culture to life at our fingertips. That game is Honor of Kings.

Thank you.
