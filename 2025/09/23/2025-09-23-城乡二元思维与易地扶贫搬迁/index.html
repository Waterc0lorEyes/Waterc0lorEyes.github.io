城乡二元思维与易地扶贫搬迁——城镇偏好的话语分析 Urban-Rural Dualism and Poverty Alleviation Relocation

2025-09-23 · 翻译作业

本文是一篇中译英翻译练习，原文探讨了城乡二元思维对易地扶贫搬迁安置模式的影响。文档包含原文（中文）、译文（英文）与翻译批注。

（一）城乡二元思维左右下搬迁选择的城镇偏好

原文

（一）城乡二元思维左右下搬迁选择的城镇偏好

译文

(1) Urban Preference in Relocation Choices Under the Influence of Urban-Rural Dualistic Thinking

批注

选择——在搬迁选择偏好中不是”选择”而是”偏好”。选择是决策行为，偏好是倾向。

Mind and matter。mind-matter dualism。pole extreme。

Under…在……下，受影响的意思。

原文

计划经济时期形成的城乡二元对立格局，在新的发展阶段其紧张程度有所舒缓，但实践中这种城乡二元思维仍旧根深蒂固。

译文

The urban-rural dualistic structure formed during the planned economy period has eased to some extent in the new development stage, yet in practice, this urban-rural dualistic mindset remains deeply entrenched.

批注

“这种城乡二元思维”重复出现可译为this mind。duality：二元性、二重性。

原文

从经济发展水平来看，G县无疑属于欠发达区域。

译文

From the perspective of economic development, County G undoubtedly falls under the category of an underdeveloped region.

批注

英文表达注意，G县为G County。Region有点大。

原文

在县域范围内，由于总体性贫困的客观存在，城乡之间的差距其实并不明显，尤其是集镇与农村之间。

译文

Within the county, due to the objective existence of overall poverty, the gap between urban and rural areas is not particularly pronounced, especially between towns and rural villages.

原文

已有研究者指出，随着基础设施的完善，技术的不断发展，以及社会流动的加剧，城乡差别正在缩小。

译文

Existing research has pointed out that as infrastructure improves, technology continues to advance, and social mobility intensifies, the urban-rural divide is narrowing.

批注

从……改用同义词替换。

原文

但即便如此，在易地扶贫搬迁向城镇集中模式的形成过程中，我们依然可以清晰地看到，在城乡二元思维的作用下，政策设计者对城镇偏好的由衷表达。

译文

Nevertheless, in the formation of the concentrated urban resettlement model for poverty alleviation through relocation, it is still evident that policy designers sincerely express a preference for urban areas under the influence of urban-rural dualistic thinking.

批注

把”城镇中”的意思加进去，要强调”城镇”。着重颠倒。Centralized注意词。

原文

如果说，资金与土地的约束是易地扶贫搬迁向城镇集中的结构性条件，那么城乡二元思维则是形塑这一模式的意识形态基础。

译文

If the constraints of funding and land are the structural conditions for concentrating poverty alleviation relocation in urban areas, then urban-rural dualistic thinking serves as the ideological foundation shaping this model.

批注

模式mode，模型model。Poverty-stricken。Disaster/flood/earthquake relief——救灾。

原文

受这一理念的影响，城镇特别是县城在搬迁安置中被赋予多重意义，对贫困人口的脱贫发挥着不可替代的作用。

译文

Influenced by this concept, urban areas, particularly county towns, are endowed with multiple significances in relocation and resettlement, playing an irreplaceable role in helping impoverished populations escape poverty.

批注

脱贫用短语shake off。

原文

首先，贫困人口向城镇集中意味着他们能够近距离与各种产业体系对接，获得更多的就业机会，以此来改善他们的生计状况。

译文

First, concentrating impoverished populations in urban areas means they can connect more closely with various industrial systems, gaining more job opportunities to improve their livelihoods.

原文

2017年，G县在新一轮的易地扶贫搬迁安置点的遴选中，其中有2个被安排在县城新建的开发区旁边。这里不仅是产业集聚地，也是重要的交通枢纽，未来这些区域有望成为县域经济发展的重要增长极。

译文

In 2017, among the newly-selected resettlement sites of the relocation project for poverty alleviation through relocation in County G, two were located near the newly developed industrial zone in the county town. This area is not only an industrial cluster but also a crucial transportation hub, expected to become a significant growth pole for the county’s economic development.

原文

“近水楼台先得月”，凭借工业中心的区位优势，被安置在此的贫困人口自然可以享受经济发展带来的益处。

译文

“Being near the water gives one an advantage.” With the locational benefits of the industrial center, the impoverished populations resettled here can naturally enjoy the benefits brought by economic development.

批注

sb. benefits from sth. / sth. benefits somebody / sth. brings benefit to sb.

原文

其次，对贫困人口来说，从偏远的村寨搬迁至城镇，客观上增加了公共服务资源的可及性。

译文

Second, for impoverished populations, relocating from remote villages to urban areas objectively increases their accessibility to public service resources.

批注

“可及性”用the accessibility。加”他们的”。

原文

他们能够更加均等化地享受城镇的教育、医疗等公共服务，进而从源头上阻断贫困的代际传递。

译文

They can enjoy more equitable access to public services such as education and healthcare in urban areas, thereby breaking the intergenerational transmission of poverty at its source.

原文

最后，政府认为，贫困人口本身也有强烈的市民化动力。

译文

Finally, the government believes that impoverished populations themselves have a strong motivation for urbanization.

原文

因此，向城镇集中搬迁不是政府的”一厢情愿”，它有着广泛的民意基础。

译文

Therefore, concentrating relocation in urban areas is not a “wishful thinking” of the government but has a broad foundation of public support.

批注

动力用gear或者pivot。

原文

受制于土地、资金等结构性条件的限制以及城乡二元思维的支配，G县易地扶贫搬迁模式逐渐从多点布局的分散安置向城镇集中安置转型。

译文

Constrained by structural conditions such as land and funding, and driven by urban-rural dualistic thinking, the poverty alleviation relocation model in County G has gradually shifted from scattered multi-point resettlement to concentrated urban resettlement.

批注

“受制于”——Owing to / Due to / Subject to。拆分可以不用那么零散。Mode模式、model使用不当。

原文

在此过程中，作为就近安置重点层级的中心村被取消，新一轮安置点的选址全部限定在县城和集镇。

译文

In this process, central villages, which were key for nearby resettlement, were eliminated, and the selection of new resettlement sites was limited entirely to county towns and townships.

原文

在新型城镇化的背景下，地方政府将城镇集中安置模式视为拉动经济增长的重要引擎、促进贫困人口脱贫致富的关键举措。

译文

Against the backdrop of new urbanization, local governments view the concentrated urban resettlement model as an important engine for driving economic growth and a key measure to help impoverished populations escape poverty and achieve prosperity.

原文

因此，此一模式负载着以就业增收、公共服务均等化等为核心内容的政策期许。

译文

Therefore, this model carries policy expectations centered on employment, income growth, and equalization of public services.

原文

不过，政策的逻辑并不能替代实践的逻辑。

译文

However, the logic of policy cannot replace the logic of practice.

原文

易地扶贫搬迁的城镇集中模式将贫困人口抽象为均质化群体，进而以此为基础，对其进行单一化的政策干预，企图实现一致性的政策目标。

译文

The concentrated urban resettlement model for poverty alleviation through relocation abstracts impoverished populations as a homogeneous group, and based on this, implements oversimplified policy interventions in an attempt to achieve uniform policy goals.

批注

“单一化的”——over-simplified / one-size-fits-all。reduce to改为。sweep一刀切的。

原文

但问题是，资本在全世界每一个角落里自由流动，但我们凭什么期待每个国家或地区都发生相同的转型，而成为城镇化的受益者呢？

译文

But the question is, while capital flows freely to every corner of the world, why should we expect every country or region to undergo the same transformation and become beneficiaries of urbanization?

原文

所以，向城镇集中的搬迁安置方式在实际操作中不可避免地暴露出诸种局限。

译文

Therefore, the concentrated urban resettlement approach inevitably reveals various limitations in practice.

批注

be bound to emerge/surface——一定会浮现/暴露。
