中海油企业新闻五则汉英翻译 Five CNOOC Corporate News Translations

2025-12-21 · 翻译实践

本文翻译了五则中国海洋石油总公司的企业新闻，包含原文、译文及翻译批注。

1. 中国海油与道达尔签署液化天然气合作协议

原文

当地时间3月26日，中国海洋石油总公司与法国道达尔公司在法国巴黎签署了《液化天然气合作协议》。

译文

CNOOC and Total Sign LNG Cooperation Agreement. On March 26 local time, China National Offshore Oil Corporation (CNOOC) and France’s Total signed an LNG cooperation agreement in Paris, France.

原文

中国国家主席习近平和法国总统奥朗德出席了签字仪式。

译文

The signing ceremony was attended by Chinese President Xi Jinping and French President François Hollande.

原文

这份协议是中法两国之间此次签署的系列协议之一。

译文

The agreement is one of a series of deals signed between China and France during this occasion.

原文

中国海油和道达尔希望在中国天然气市场再增加每年100万吨长期LNG资源供应，以及加强在LNG产业链的全面合作。

译文

CNOOC and Total aim to further expand their cooperation by adding an annual supply of 1 million tons of long-term LNG resources to the Chinese natural gas market, while also strengthening comprehensive collaboration across the LNG industry chain.

原文

自2008年中国海油与道达尔签署每年100万吨长期LNG资源购销协议以来，双方合作良好。

译文

Since the two companies signed a long-term LNG sales and purchase agreement for 1 million tons per year in 2008, their partnership has progressed smoothly.

原文

签署并执行了一系列中短期和现货LNG协议。

译文

They have since signed and executed a series of medium- and short-term LNG agreements, as well as spot LNG deals.

原文

截至目前，道达尔已向中国海油交付了总量超过500万吨的LNG资源。

译文

To date, Total has delivered a total of over 5 million tons of LNG resources to CNOOC.

2. 中国海油发明专利授权量首进国内企业前十

原文

3月24日，从中国海洋石油总公司科技发展部获悉。

译文

CNOOC Ranks Among Top 10 Domestic Enterprises in Invention Patent Grants. On March 24, it was learned from CNOOC’s Science and Technology Development Department.

原文

在国家知识产权局近日公布的2013年国内企业（不含港澳台）发明专利授权量排行中，中国海油以275项发明专利授权量首次进入国内企业发明专利授权量前十位，位列第十。

译文

According to the recent ranking of invention patent grants for domestic enterprises (not including Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan) in 2013 released by the National Intellectual Property Administration, CNOOC entered the top 10 list for the first time, ranking tenth with 275 invention patents granted.

批注

not include的使用更合适，exclude有排除在外的意思偏贬义不合适。

原文

2013年，总公司共获得国内专利授权708项，其中，发明专利275项。

译文

In 2013, the corporation was granted a total of 708 domestic patents, of which 275 were invention patents.

原文

发明专利主要集中在上游板块，主要来源于研究总院、中海油服、海油发展和海油工程等单位。

译文

The invention patents were primarily concentrated in the upstream sector, mainly originating from entities such as the Research Institute, COSL, CNOOC Energy Technology & Services Limited, and COOEC.

批注

来源太重。

原文

在前十位的榜单上，华为技术有限公司以2251项的发明专利授权量位居首位。

译文

On the top 10 list, Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. ranked first with 2,251 invention patents granted.

原文

中国石化和中国石油以1627项和527项的发明专利授权量分列第二位和第四位。

译文

Sinopec and CNPC ranked second and fourth with 1,627 and 527 invention patents granted, respectively.

3. 中国海油再获”中国儿童慈善奖”

原文

全国妇联和中国儿童少年基金会3月18日召开2013年度”中国儿童慈善奖”表彰会。

译文

CNOOC Wins “China Children’s Charity Award” Again. The All-China Women’s Federation and the China Children and Teenagers’ Fund held the 2013 “China Children’s Charity Award” recognition ceremony on March 18.

原文

其中，中国海油等49个单位和李家诚等12名个人被授予”中国儿童慈善奖——突出贡献奖”。

译文

Among the honorees, CNOOC and 48 other organizations, as well as 12 individuals including Sir Li Ka-shing, were awarded the “China Children’s Charity Award - Outstanding Contribution Award”.

批注

李家诚——Sir Li Ka-shing。

原文

中国海油长期关注支持中国妇女儿童事业的发展。

译文

CNOOC has long been committed to supporting the development of initiatives for Chinese women and children.

原文

在母亲水窖、希望工程、消除婴幼儿贫血行动和支持中国妇女儿童博物馆建设等多项工作中参与支持。

译文

The company has participated in and supported various projects, including the Mother Water Cellar project, Project Hope, the Action to Eliminate Infant Anemia, and the construction of the National Museum of Chinese Women and Children.

批注

动词有点多，可以删除一些动词。

原文

本次荣获”中国儿童慈善奖——突出贡献奖”是继2011年后，中国海油再次获此荣誉。

译文

This award marks the second time CNOOC has received this honor since 2011.

4. 中国海油参展第14届中国国际石油石化技术装备展览会

原文

3月19日，第14届中国国际石油石化技术装备展览会在京开展，中国海油参展。

译文

CNOOC Participates in the 14th China International Petroleum & Petrochemical Technology and Equipment Exhibition. The 14th China International Petroleum & Petrochemical Technology and Equipment Exhibition opened in Beijing on March 19, with CNOOC participating.

原文

中国工程院院士周守为在同期举办的以”技术革新与新能源”为主题的第六届国际石油产业高峰论坛上致辞。

译文

Zhou Shouwei, an academician of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, delivered a speech at the concurrently held 6th International Petroleum Industry Summit Forum, which was themed “Technological Innovation and New Energy”.

原文

本届展会共举办三天，吸引了65个国家和地区的1600多个企业参展。

译文

The 3-day CIPPE exhibition attracted more than 1,600 enterprises from 65 countries and regions.

批注

三天可以放在前面更好。

原文

众多展商在石油石化技术装备、页岩气技术装备、海洋石油天然气技术装备、管道与储运技术装备、防爆电气设备及海事技术装备等6大板块展示最新的产品和技术。

译文

Numerous exhibitors showcased their latest products and technologies across six major sectors: petroleum and petrochemical technology equipment, shale gas technology equipment, offshore oil and gas technology equipment, pipeline and storage/transportation technology equipment, explosion-proof electrical equipment, and maritime technology equipment.

原文

此次海工装备企业占总参展商的数量达到了40%。

译文

Offshore engineering equipment enterprises accounted for 40% of the total exhibitors.

原文

国内外企业新近研发制造的海洋防喷器、水下采油树、海洋钻井隔水管系统、高压计量泵等满足深海油气开发需求的海工装备产品纷纷亮相此次展会。

译文

Newly developed offshore equipment products from domestic and international companies, such as marine blowout preventers, subsea Christmas trees, offshore drilling riser systems, and high-pressure metering pumps designed to meet deepwater oil and gas development needs, were unveiled at the exhibition.

5. 中海油国际融资租赁公司成立 & “加拿大企业走进中国”合作研讨会

原文

3月7日，中海油国际融资租赁公司正式成立。

译文

CNOOC International Financial Leasing Company Established. On March 7, CNOOC International Financial Leasing Company was officially incorporated.

批注

公司成立——be incorporated。

原文

中海油国际融资租赁公司注册地为天津市东疆保税港区。

译文

The company is registered in the Dongjiang Free Trade Port Zone of Tianjin.

原文

该公司是一家以拓展石油装备、海工装备、石油工具、炼化装置、工业成套、通用机电、安全节能节水及环保、航空运输、轨道交通等设备租赁业务为主的融资租赁公司。

译文

It is a financial leasing company primarily engaged in expanding equipment leasing businesses for petroleum equipment, offshore engineering equipment, petroleum tools, refining and chemical installations, industrial complete sets, general electromechanical equipment, safety, energy/water conservation and environmental protection, air transportation, and rail transit equipment.

原文

该公司的成立是中国海洋石油总公司产贸融相结合的重要举措。

译文

The establishment of this company is a significant move by CNOOC to integrate industry, trade, and finance.

原文

是总公司境内境外融资的重要平台。

译文

It serves as an important platform for CNOOC’s domestic and international financing.

原文

3月17日，由中国海洋石油有限公司主办的”加拿大企业走进中国”合作研讨会在京举行。

译文

CNOOC Hosts “Canadian Enterprises Enter China” Cooperation Seminar. On March 17, the “Canadian Enterprises Enter China” cooperation seminar, hosted by CNOOC Ltd., was held in Beijing.

批注

动词多，需要删减。

原文

来自34家加拿大油气行业服务企业的近70位代表参加活动。

译文

Nearly 70 representatives from 34 Canadian oil and gas service companies participated in the event.

原文

他们将在一周内参加由中海油组织的近60个一对一的会见。

译文

They were scheduled to participate in nearly 60 one-on-one meetings organized by CNOOC over the course of a week.

原文

以期建立与中国石油、中国石化、中国海油、华能等中国能源企业的合作。

译文

The aim was to establish cooperation with Chinese energy enterprises such as CNPC, Sinopec, CNOOC, and China Huaneng.

原文

“加拿大企业走进中国”活动旨在与加拿大中小企业分享在华发展的机会。

译文

The “Canadian Enterprises Enter China” event aims to share development opportunities in China with Canadian small and medium-sized enterprises.

原文

促进中加企业在全球范围内合作。

译文

and to promote cooperation between Chinese and Canadian enterprises globally.

原文

这也是中海油兑现收购尼克森时所作承诺的重要举措。

译文

This is also an important measure for CNOOC to fulfill the commitments made during its acquisition of Nexen.

原文

中海油希望通过此次活动，为中国能源企业与加拿大中小企业搭建交流的桥梁。

译文

CNOOC hopes that this event will build a communication bridge between Chinese energy enterprises and Canadian SMEs.
