中海油企业新闻五则汉英翻译 CNOOC Corporate News Translation

2025-12-21 · 翻译实践

本文翻译了五则中国海油企业简讯，包含五则中国海油（CNOOC）企业简讯的原文与译文。

一、中国海油与道达尔签署液化天然气合作协议

原文

当地时间3月26日，中国海洋石油总公司与法国道达尔公司在法国巴黎签署了《液化天然气合作协议》。中国国家主席习近平和法国总统奥朗德出席了签字仪式。这份协议是中法两国之间此次签署的系列协议之一。中国海油和道达尔希望在中国天然气市场再增加每年100万吨长期LNG资源供应，以及加强在LNG产业链的全面合作。自2008年中国海油与道达尔签署每年100万吨长期LNG资源购销协议以来，双方合作良好。签署并执行了一系列中短期和现货LNG协议。截至目前，道达尔已向中国海油交付了总量超过500万吨的LNG资源。

译文

On March 26 local time, China National Offshore Oil Corporation (CNOOC) and France’s Total signed an LNG cooperation agreement in Paris, France. The signing ceremony was attended by Chinese President Xi Jinping and French President François Hollande. The agreement is one of a series of deals signed between China and France during this occasion. CNOOC and Total aim to further expand their cooperation by adding an annual supply of 1 million tons of long-term LNG resources to the Chinese natural gas market, while also strengthening comprehensive collaboration across the LNG industry chain. Since the two companies signed a long-term LNG sales and purchase agreement for 1 million tons per year in 2008, their partnership has progressed smoothly. They have since signed and executed a series of medium- and short-term LNG agreements, as well as spot LNG deals. To date, Total has delivered a total of over 5 million tons of LNG resources to CNOOC.

二、中国海油发明专利授权量首进国内企业前十

原文

3月24日，从中国海洋石油总公司科技发展部获悉，在国家知识产权局近日公布的2013年国内企业（不含港澳台）发明专利授权量排行中，中国海油以275项发明专利授权量首次进入国内企业发明专利授权量前十位，位列第十。2013年，总公司共获得国内专利授权708项，其中，发明专利275项。发明专利主要集中在上游板块，主要来源于研究总院、中海油服、海油发展和海油工程等单位。在前十位的榜单上，华为技术有限公司以2251项的发明专利授权量位居首位。中国石化和中国石油以1627项和527项的发明专利授权量分列第二位和第四位。

译文

On March 24, it was learned from CNOOC’s Science and Technology Development Department that, according to the ranking of invention patent grants for domestic enterprises (not including Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan) in 2013 released by the National Intellectual Property Administration, CNOOC entered the top 10 list for the first time, ranking tenth with 275 invention patents granted. In 2013, the corporation was granted a total of 708 domestic patents, of which 275 were invention patents. The invention patents were primarily concentrated in the upstream sector, mainly originating from entities such as the Research Institute, COSL, CNOOC Energy Technology & Services Limited, and COOEC. On the top 10 list, Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. ranked first with 2,251 invention patents granted. Sinopec and CNPC ranked second and fourth with 1,627 and 527 invention patents granted, respectively.

三、中国海油再获”中国儿童慈善奖”

原文

全国妇联和中国儿童少年基金会3月18日召开2013年度”中国儿童慈善奖”表彰会。其中，中国海油等49个单位和李家诚等12名个人被授予”中国儿童慈善奖——突出贡献奖”。中国海油长期关注支持中国妇女儿童事业的发展，在母亲水窖、希望工程、消除婴幼儿贫血行动和支持中国妇女儿童博物馆建设等多项工作中参与支持。本次获奖是继2011年后中国海油再次获此荣誉。

译文

The All-China Women’s Federation and the China Children and Teenagers’ Fund held the 2013 “China Children’s Charity Award” recognition ceremony on March 18. Among the honorees, CNOOC and 48 other organizations, as well as 12 individuals including Sir Li Ka-shing, were awarded the “China Children’s Charity Award - Outstanding Contribution Award”. CNOOC has long been committed to supporting the development of initiatives for Chinese women and children, participating in and supporting various projects including the Mother Water Cellar project, Project Hope, the Action to Eliminate Infant Anemia, and the construction of the National Museum of Chinese Women and Children. This award marks the second time CNOOC has received this honor since 2011.

四、中国海油参展第14届中国国际石油石化技术装备展览会

原文

3月19日，第14届中国国际石油石化技术装备展览会在京开展，中国海油参展。中国工程院院士周守为在同期举办的以”技术革新与新能源”为主题的第六届国际石油产业高峰论坛上致辞。本届展会共举办三天，吸引了65个国家和地区的1600多个企业参展。众多展商在石油石化技术装备、页岩气技术装备、海洋石油天然气技术装备、管道与储运技术装备、防爆电气设备及海事技术装备等6大板块展示最新的产品和技术。此次海工装备企业占总参展商的数量达到了40%。国内外企业新近研发制造的海洋防喷器、水下采油树、海洋钻井隔水管系统、高压计量泵等满足深海油气开发需求的海工装备产品纷纷亮相此次展会。

译文

The 14th China International Petroleum & Petrochemical Technology and Equipment Exhibition opened in Beijing on March 19, with CNOOC participating. Zhou Shouwei, an academician of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, delivered a speech at the concurrently held 6th International Petroleum Industry Summit Forum, themed “Technological Innovation and New Energy”. The three-day exhibition attracted over 1,600 enterprises from 65 countries and regions. Numerous exhibitors showcased their latest products and technologies across six major sectors: petroleum and petrochemical technology equipment, shale gas technology equipment, offshore oil and gas technology equipment, pipeline and storage/transportation technology equipment, explosion-proof electrical equipment, and maritime technology equipment. Offshore engineering equipment enterprises accounted for 40% of the total exhibitors. Newly developed offshore equipment products from domestic and international companies, such as marine blowout preventers, subsea Christmas trees, offshore drilling riser systems, and high-pressure metering pumps designed to meet deepwater oil and gas development needs, were unveiled at the exhibition.

五、中海油国际融资租赁公司成立

原文

3月7日，中海油国际融资租赁公司正式成立，注册地为天津市东疆保税港区。该公司是一家以拓展石油装备、海工装备、石油工具、炼化装置、工业成套、通用机电、安全节能节水及环保、航空运输、轨道交通等设备租赁业务为主的融资租赁公司。该公司的成立是中国海洋石油总公司产贸融相结合的重要举措，是总公司境内境外融资的重要平台。

译文

On March 7, CNOOC International Financial Leasing Company was officially incorporated, registered in the Dongjiang Free Trade Port Zone of Tianjin. It is a financial leasing company primarily engaged in expanding equipment leasing businesses for petroleum equipment, offshore engineering equipment, petroleum tools, refining and chemical installations, industrial complete sets, general electromechanical equipment, safety, energy/water conservation and environmental protection, air transportation, and rail transit equipment. The establishment of this company is a significant move by CNOOC to integrate industry, trade, and finance. It serves as an important platform for CNOOC’s domestic and international financing.

译者注： 企业新闻翻译需注意：动词精简（中文多用动词堆砌，英语需合并为复合句）；”not including” 优于 “excluding”（前者中性客观）；”achievement” 少用于描述负面现象；展期时长类时间状语宜前置；”subsea Christmas trees” 为行业术语，指水下采油树装置。
