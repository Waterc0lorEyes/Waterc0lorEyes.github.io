口译听写反思——如何科学饮水

2025-01-20 · 翻译作业

本文是一篇口译听写练习的英文发言稿，主题为科学饮水的常识与建议，并附练习后的自我复盘总结。

英文发言稿

Today I’m going to talk about something about drinking water. We all know the water is very important for people. 70% of the earth surface is covered by water and about 70% of a person’s body weight is water. So for our people, we couldn’t live without water. If our body are lack in water, we will have some problems. For example, your short term memory becomes very bad or it is very difficult to have some simple remember, just like some simple words or numbers. Since water is so important for people, how should we drink water scientifically?

So the first question is what to drink. For example, we could drink coffee, tea or some juice. Some of them tastes good, but some have some special effects. For example, coffee could keep us not to sleep. However, lots of people like to…they like to only drink these drinks, but they could lead to some…some bad endings. For example, I have a friend who only drinks coffee and but nothing else. But in fact, this is very not…not good for us. For our health, we could drink more water. And we have two choice to drink water, the tap water and bottle water. I recommend us to drink more tap water, because it is more cheap, more convenient and more environmental friendly than the bottle water.

总结复盘

尽管能够理解并表达句子的意思，但缺少细节的准确性。比如单复数的不注意导致了主谓不一致，所有格的缺失，词语词性的使用错误等等，仍然需要在细节处多加注意。

在陈述过程中容易出现紧张的现象。大脑空白会使句子不连贯，在思考之后也容易出现习惯性的”呃”，思考下一句要说什么的时候还会对单词进行重复，听起来很没有自信。此时应该专注于下一句该说的话，不能纠结刚才说过的正确与否，否则会导致后面的接连紧张。

句子的表达容易出现混乱。比如会说出一些中式的表达，或者是习惯性的说出来比较混乱的逻辑的句子，会让人觉得不专业。应该减少中式思维到句子中的使用，让句子更连贯自然。
