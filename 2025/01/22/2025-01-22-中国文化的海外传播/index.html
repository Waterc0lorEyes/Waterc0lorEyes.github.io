中国文化的海外传播——近年来现状与展望

2025-01-22 · 翻译作业

近二十年来，中国文化的海外传播取得了显著进展。从孔子学院的全球布局到春节被多国认可为国际节日，从功夫电影到网络文学——中国文化正以更加多元的形式走向世界舞台。本文翻译了一篇关于中国文化海外传播现状的英文综述。

The Situation of Chinese Culture Dissemination Abroad in Recent Years

原文

Over the past two decades, the overseas dissemination of Chinese culture has achieved remarkable progress, with its influence gradually expanding. This process has benefited from the acceleration of globalization and the enhancement of China’s comprehensive national strength, while the channels and forms of cultural communication have also become increasingly diversified.

译文

近二十年来，中国文化的海外传播取得了显著进展，其影响力逐步扩大。这一进程得益于全球化的加速推进和中国综合国力的不断增强，文化交流的渠道与形式也日益多元化。

原文

Firstly, language dissemination has become a critical pathway for cultural outreach. With the rise of “Mandarin fever,” the number of global Mandarin learners has continued to grow. As of 2023, Confucius Institutes and Confucius Classrooms have been established in over 180 countries and regions, serving as vital platforms for promoting the Chinese language and culture. The international status of Mandarin has significantly improved, laying a foundational role in cultural dissemination.

译文

首先，语言传播已成为文化对外推广的关键路径。随着”汉语热”的兴起，全球汉语学习者的数量持续增长。截至2023年，孔子学院和孔子课堂已在180多个国家和地区建立，成为推广中国语言和文化的重要平台。汉语的国际地位显著提升，在文化传播中起着奠基性作用。

译者注： “Mandarin fever”译为”汉语热”保留中文媒体报道中的习惯说法。”Confucius Institutes”固定译名为”孔子学院”。”laying a foundational role”译为”起着奠基性作用”比直译”发挥着基础作用”更有力度。

原文

Secondly, traditional cultural elements have frequently featured on the global stage. Traditional arts and skills such as Peking Opera, calligraphy, martial arts, and traditional Chinese medicine have reached the world through cultural exchange activities, films, and exhibitions. For instance, the opening ceremony of the 2008 Beijing Olympics showcased China’s profound cultural heritage, becoming a landmark case in cultural dissemination. Additionally, traditional festivals like the Spring Festival are increasingly recognized by more countries, evolving into global cultural celebrations.

译文

其次，传统文化元素频繁登上世界舞台。京剧、书法、武术、中医等传统艺术与技艺通过文化交流活动、电影和展览走向世界。例如，2008年北京奥运会开幕式展示了中国深厚的文化底蕴，成为文化传播的标志性案例。此外，春节等传统节日正被越来越多的国家所认可，逐渐演变为全球性的文化庆典。

译者注： “frequently featured”译为”频繁登上”比”频繁出现”更有主动性。”cultural heritage”译为”文化底蕴”而非直译”文化遗产”，中文读者习惯用此表达。”landmark case”译为”标志性案例”。

原文

The output of films and literary works has further accelerated cultural transmission. Chinese films such as Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon and Hero have garnered significant international attention, highlighting the unique charm of Chinese culture. Online literature and TV dramas have also reached global audiences through streaming platforms, attracting viewers in Southeast Asia and Western regions.

译文

电影与文学作品的输出进一步加速了文化传播。中国电影如《卧虎藏龙》《英雄》在国际上获得了广泛关注，彰显了中国文化的独特魅力。网络文学和电视剧也通过流媒体平台触达全球观众，在东南亚和西方地区吸引了大量受众。

译者注： “garnered significant international attention”译为”获得了广泛关注”，简洁有力。”streaming platforms”译为”流媒体平台”是行业通用术语。

原文

However, cultural dissemination still faces challenges. Cultural differences and stereotypes persist, and some Western media interpretations of Chinese culture remain biased. Furthermore, the depth and breadth of cultural dissemination require improvement, as many foreigners’ understanding of Chinese culture remains superficial.

译文

然而，文化传播仍面临挑战。文化差异与刻板印象依然存在，部分西方媒体对中国文化的解读仍带有偏见。此外，文化传播的深度和广度仍有待提升，许多外国人对中国文化的理解仍停留在表面。

译者注： “biased”译为”带有偏见”比”有偏见的”更符合中文表达习惯。”superficial”译为”停留在表面”比”肤浅”更中性客观。

原文

Overall, the overseas dissemination of Chinese culture over the past two decades has achieved notable results. Nevertheless, future efforts should focus on innovating dissemination methods and deepening cultural connotations to enhance China’s global cultural influence.

译文

总体而言，中国文化在过去二十年间的海外传播取得了显著成效。然而，未来的努力应聚焦于创新传播方式、深化文化内涵，以提升中国在全球范围内的文化影响力。
