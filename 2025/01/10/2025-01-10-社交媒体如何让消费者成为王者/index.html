社交媒体如何让消费者成为王者

2025-01-10 · 翻译作业

信息即权力。本文探讨了社交媒体如何彻底改变了商业运作模式——信息不再被少数人垄断，消费者从被动的信息接收者转变为主动的”信息型买家”，企业被迫前所未有地倾听消费者的声音。

How Social Media has Made the Consumer King

原文

Information is power. Quite simply, those who control information have power over others. For millennia, power was in the hands of the few—those individuals in society who controlled both the flow of information and peoples’ access to it. Today, we are in the midst of a revolution; a revolution brought on by the power of information-sharing. Today, no one person or group has a monopoly on information; information is owned by everyone. What has this meant for businesses? It has meant that they have had to start listening to consumers like never before.

译文

信息即权力。简而言之，掌控信息者便能支配他人。数千年来，权力始终掌握在少数人手中——那些既控制信息流向又垄断获取渠道的社会个体。如今，我们正处于一场由信息共享力量引发的革命之中。当今时代，没有任何个人或团体能独占信息；信息属于全体大众。这对企业意味着什么？这意味着他们必须前所未有地倾听消费者的声音。

译者注： “For millennia”译为”数千年来”保留时间跨度感；”a monopoly on information”译为”独占信息”，比”垄断”更口语化。

原文

In the past, consumers learned about products and services when marketers and advertisers created messages that were then pushed through traditional mass-media channels. Information, in other words, was one-directional; the message was fully controlled by the brand. Thanks to the emergence of user-generated content and social media, businesses are no longer in complete control. The consumer has acquired immense power. Their voice is now everywhere. In 2020 alone, UK consumers left well over 200 million comments online. The power of digital opinion is now mightier than the power of the advertising dollar.

译文

过去，消费者只能通过营销人员和广告商精心设计、再经由传统大众媒体渠道推送的信息来了解产品与服务。换言之，信息是单向流动的，品牌完全掌控着话语权。随着用户发布内容和社交媒体的兴起，企业已无法继续独揽大权。消费者获得了巨大的力量，他们的声音如今无处不在。仅2020年，英国消费者就在线留下了超过2亿条评论。用户评价的影响力已超过广告投放的效果。

译者注： “one-directional”译为”单向流动”而非”单向的”，名词化更流畅；”advertising dollar”意译为”广告投放的效果”，避免直译”广告美元”让中文读者费解。

原文

Social media has changed the way people do business and influenced the entire buying process. Consumers now use social media outlets to research companies before doing business with them. According to one study, two thirds of consumers use information they find through social media to influence their buying decisions. Roughly the same percentage (67%) are then likely to pass this information on to others and more than 60% trust the information they find through social media more than they do via traditional advertisements. Customers can get feedback and price comparisons at the point of purchase, in real time, through applications, sites or postings in social networks. With consumers offering other consumers product reviews and suggestions, consumers now have the upper hand.

译文

社交媒体改变了商业运作模式，重塑了整个购买流程。如今消费者会先通过社交平台调研企业背景再决定交易。研究显示，三分之二消费者会依据社交媒体获取的信息做出购买决策，约同等比例（67%）的人会将这些信息分享给他人，超过60%的人认为社交媒体信息比传统广告更可信。借助各类应用程序、网站或社交网络动态，顾客能在购买时实时获取产品反馈和比价信息。随着消费者之间相互提供产品评测与建议，主动权已转移到消费者手中。

译者注： “have the upper hand”译为”主动权已转移”，比直译”占上风”更适合商业语境。

原文

Social media has also made consumers feel empowered to say the things they’ve always wanted to say. In one study, 20% of U.S. social media users said that they had lashed out against brands and companies online. Social media has also given rise to the “critical consumer”: consumers who are so well versed in the marketplace that they are increasingly hard to persuade. One study revealed that over half (56%) of consumers check two to three websites before purchasing an item, with 21% checking more than three websites. As disposable income is squeezed, impulsive shopping is fast becoming a thing of the past. Today’s consumer is an “information shopper”, someone who will only buy after conducting research online. Three quarters of those surveyed said they will wait “as long as it takes” to secure the best deal. And today’s consumer does not stop once their purchase has been made. 40% of those surveyed stated that they continued to monitor the product they had just bought to ensure they got value for money.

译文

社交媒体还赋予消费者畅所欲言的底气。在一项研究中，20%的美国社交媒体用户表示曾在网上抨击过品牌与企业。这也催生了”批判型消费者”——这群人对市场了如指掌，越来越难以被说服。数据显示，超半数（56%）的消费者购买前会查阅两至三个网站进行比价，21%的人会查阅超三个网站。随着消费预算收紧，冲动购物正成为历史。当代消费者是”信息型买家”，必定先在线调研再购买。75%的受访者表示愿”无限期等待”最优价格。这种谨慎态度甚至延续到购买之后——40%的消费者会持续追踪已购商品以确保物有所值。

译者注： “critical consumer”译为”批判型消费者”保留原文批判性色彩；”information shopper”译为”信息型买家”沿用中文流行术语；”as long as it takes”译为”无限期等待”简洁有力。

原文

Today having a presence on social media is an important part of any company’s marketing strategy. Companies have been forced to build a strong presence in social media sites in order to react quickly to negative feedback and customers’ demands; to develop a relationship with their customers; to influence the direction of the conversation; and, ultimately, to make contact with their customers.

译文

如今，入驻社交媒体已成为企业营销战略的重要环节。企业被迫在社交平台建立强大存在感，以快速应对负面反馈与客户需求，培养客户关系，引导舆论走向，最终实现与消费者的深度联结。

译者注： 原文四个并列不定式中，将”make contact with”译为”深度联结”而非直译”建立联系”，提升结尾力度。并列结构用分号或逗号分段处理，保持中文节奏感。
