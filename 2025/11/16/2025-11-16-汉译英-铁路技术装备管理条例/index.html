汉译英——铁路技术装备管理条例 C-E Translation: Railway Technical Equipment Regulations

2025-11-16 · 翻译作业

技术法规文本的翻译要求术语绝对精确、句式严谨规范。本文以《铁路技术装备管理条例》第一编基本要求为素材，展示了中文法规英译中的术语一致性、法律语力转换与句式重构策略。

第一编 技术设备 · 第一章 基本要求

第1条 基建、制造及其验收交接

原文

铁路的基本建设、产品制造应综合配套，保证质量，采用保证行车安全的技术装备，不断提高运输能力，以适应国民经济发展需要。应采用安全可靠、先进成熟、经济适用的技术，实现技术装备标准化、系列化、模块化、信息化，加快实现铁路现代化。

译文

Railway capital construction and product manufacturing shall be comprehensively coordinated, quality assured, and equipped with technology that ensures operational safety. The goal is to continuously enhance transport capacity to meet the needs of national economic development. Safe, reliable, advanced, mature, economically viable, and applicable technologies shall be adopted to achieve standardization, serialization, modularization, and informatization of technical equipment, thereby accelerating the modernization of railways.

第2条 建设程序

原文

铁路基本建设应严格按照国家规定的程序进行，必须符合国家相关法律法规，执行国家标准、行业标准和技术规范。设计工作由具有相应资质等级的单位承担，根据已批准的可行性研究报告进行，并充分听取相关铁路企业的意见。设计文件按项目审批部门确定的审批程序批准。

译文

Railway capital construction shall strictly adhere to the procedures stipulated by the state, comply with relevant national laws and regulations, and implement national standards, industry standards, and technical specifications. Design work shall be undertaken by units with corresponding qualification grades, based on the approved feasibility study report, and shall fully solicit opinions from relevant railway enterprises. Design documents shall be approved according to the approval procedures determined by the project approval department.

译者注： “单位”不能望文生义译为unit，应根据语境选用 department 或 section。”设计”在句中可用作动词，无需名词化。

第2A条 术语定义

原文

本规程所称机车车辆包括铁路机车、客车、货车、动车组以及自轮运转特种设备。本规程所称铁路企业是指铁路运输企业以及铁路产权单位。

译文

The term “rolling stock” (locomotives and vehicles) as used in these Regulations includes railway locomotives, passenger coaches, freight wagons, multiple units (EMUs/DMUs), and special self-propelled equipment. The term “railway enterprise” as used in these Regulations refers to railway transport enterprises and railway asset-owning units.

译者注： “自轮运转”用wheel体现带轮特征。”transport”一词全文需统一。

铁路运输企业是指从事铁路旅客、货物公共运输营业的企业法人，铁路运输企业按有关规定取得铁路运输许可证，按照许可范围开展铁路运输营业。铁路产权单位是指拥有铁路基础设施所有权的企业。铁路产权单位可按有关规定取得铁路运输许可证，也可委托其他铁路运输企业开展铁路运输营业。

译文

A railway transport enterprise refers to an enterprise legal person engaged in the business of public railway passenger and freight transport. Railway transport enterprises shall obtain a railway transport license according to relevant regulations and conduct railway transport operations within the licensed scope. A railway asset-owning unit refers to an enterprise that owns the railway infrastructure. Railway asset-owning units may obtain a railway transport license according to relevant regulations, or may entrust other railway transport enterprises to conduct railway transport operations.

译者注： “营业”译为service或business更合适，比operation更贴近运输服务行业的语境。

第3条 工程施工

原文

工程施工按照批准的设计文件的要求进行，严格执行工程建设项目招投标和监理制度。相关铁路企业会同工程设计、施工单位制定安全施工方案，按照方案进行施工，加强环境保护，确保工程质量。施工完毕应及时清理现场，不得影响铁路运营安全。涉及营业线施工时，应按铁路企业规定程序审批，且保证行车安全，减少对运输的影响。

译文

Engineering construction shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of the approved design documents, strictly implementing the bidding and supervision systems for construction projects. The relevant railway enterprises shall, in conjunction with the engineering design and construction units, formulate a safe construction plan, construct according to the plan, strengthen environmental protection, and ensure project quality. Upon completion of construction, the site shall be promptly cleaned up without affecting the safety of railway operations. When construction involves existing railway lines, it shall be approved according to the procedures stipulated by the railway enterprise, ensuring traffic safety and minimizing the impact on transportation.

译者注： 中文原文动词较多（”进行””执行””制定””按照””加强””确保””清理”），英文译文应尽量减少动词，转化为介词短语、分词结构或名词化表达，以符合英文法规文本的严谨简洁风格。

第4条 验收交接

原文

新建、改建工程竣工后，应按规定进行验收和安全评估。线路验收时应达到设计速度。在确认工程符合技术标准、设计文件的要求，并检查竣工文件和技术设备使用说明书等资料齐全后，方可交接。新建、改建的工程设施有明确的质量保证期。如运输生产急需，可按上述原则分段验收交接。

译文

Upon completion of new or renovated projects, acceptance checks and safety assessments shall be conducted in accordance with regulations. During track acceptance, the design speed shall be achieved. The handover can only take place after verifying that the project complies with technical standards and the requirements of the design documents, and confirming that the completion documents and technical equipment manuals are complete. New or renovated engineering facilities shall have a clearly defined warranty period. If urgently needed for transport operations, sectional acceptance and handover may be conducted in accordance with the above principles.

译者注： 需强调线路验收与设计速度的关系，不能遗漏”分段验收”（sectional acceptance）的翻译。

第5~13条 技术设备管理

原文（摘要）

第5条：铁路基本建设项目中的环境保护、节能、水土保持、劳动安全、职业卫生、消防、安全防护、公共安全等设施，和主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产。

第6条：铁路产品应符合国家和铁道行业技术标准。铁路重要产品按有关规定，执行行政许可、产品认证等铁路产品准入制度。

第7条：铁路企业应严格控制进入铁路的产品质量，建立必要的产品质量检验和质量问题追究制度。制造、检修的机车车辆及其重要配件经铁路企业或其委托的专业机构监造，符合要求后，方准交付使用。

第8条：新设备投入使用前有操作规程、竣工图纸等技术文件和保证安全生产的办法与管理细则，经过技术测验合格并对有关人员进行培训后，方可使用。

第9条：铁路机车车辆、线路、桥隧、通信、信号、牵引供电、电力、信息、安全、给水、房建等技术设备，均有完整和正确反映其技术状态的文件及技术履历等有关资料。上述技术资料由有关部门或单位妥善保管，并根据变化情况及时记载修订。

第10条：机车车辆等技术设备应有行业统一规定的标记。隐蔽的建（构）筑物及设备应在地面上设有标志。

第11条：铁路企业应制定机车、客车、货车、动车组等主要设备的资产技术管理制度。机车车辆重大技术改变等涉及行政许可管理的事项，应按规定办理相应的行政许可手续。

第12条：高原铁路应考虑高寒缺氧特点，为旅客和工作人员提供必要的救助设施，应采用高可靠、少维修、远程监控的运输设备。

第13条：对现有不符合本规程规定标准的技术设备，有条件的应逐步改造或更换，未改造完之前铁路企业应制定安全措施。

译文

Article 5: Environmental protection, energy conservation, soil and water conservation, labor safety, occupational health, fire protection, safety precautions, public safety, and other facilities within railway capital construction projects shall be designed, constructed, and put into operation simultaneously with the main project.

Article 6: Railway products shall comply with national and railway industry technical standards. Important railway products shall implement railway product access systems, such as administrative licensing and product certification, in accordance with relevant regulations.

Article 7: Railway enterprises shall strictly control the quality of products entering the railway sector and establish necessary product quality inspection and quality issue tracking systems. Manufactured or overhauled locomotives, rolling stock, and their important components can only be delivered for use after being inspected by the railway enterprise or a professional institution authorized by it and confirmed to meet requirements.

Article 8: Before new equipment is put into use, there shall be operational procedures, as-built drawings, and other technical documents, as well as measures and management rules to ensure safety. It can only be used after passing technical tests and the completion of training for relevant personnel.

Article 9: Technical equipment such as railway locomotives and rolling stock, tracks, bridges and tunnels, communications, signaling, traction power supply, power systems, information systems, safety systems, water supply, and buildings shall have complete and accurate documentation reflecting their technical status. The relevant departments or units shall properly keep the aforementioned technical documentation and update it promptly based on changes.

Article 10: Technical equipment such as locomotives and rolling stock shall bear the uniformly prescribed markings within the industry. Concealed structures and equipment shall have above-ground markers.

Article 11: Railway enterprises shall establish asset technical management systems for major equipment such as locomotives, passenger coaches, freight wagons, and multiple units. For significant technical modifications involving administrative licensing management, the corresponding procedures shall be completed.

Article 12: Plateau railways shall take into account the characteristics of high altitude, cold temperatures, and hypoxia, providing necessary rescue facilities for passengers and staff. Transportation equipment with high reliability, low maintenance requirements, and remote monitoring capabilities shall be adopted.

Article 13: For existing technical equipment that does not comply with the standards specified in these Regulations, gradual upgrades or replacements shall be carried out where conditions permit. Prior to the completion of such upgrades, railway enterprises shall formulate safety measures.
